# The age clock decides your ANP entry

A computer science student or graduate in Algeria has five realistic ways into the Ministry of National Defence (MDN). What decides which ones are open is your age on 31 December of the application year, not your technical skill. The main way in is the annual intake of **career officer cadets recruited on their degree ("élèves officiers de carrière sur titre")**. It is open to single Algerians whose age is at most 19 plus the minimum years of study for their diploma, which means about **22 with a Licence and 24 with a Master or Ingénieur d'État**. Candidates pre-register online at preinscription.mdn.dz in early August (2 August in 2026), sit a competition and train for about two years, starting at the Cherchell military academy. The Gendarmerie and the Garde Républicaine recruit graduate officers through the same intake. Contract NCO and rank-and-file posts are open to anyone aged 18–22, but they give no credit for a degree. Older graduates, married graduates and anyone who wants a civilian job can compete for **personnels civils assimilés (PCA)** posts in informatique. The 2022 age caps were 35 or 40, but you must have settled your national service and live in the wilaya of the post. National service is 12 months and compulsory for men from 19, and studies can defer it. It is an obligation, not a career route, although signing on directly and serving 24 months counts in its place. The well-known schools that train the ANP's IT officers (Cherchell's informatique track, the École Supérieure des Transmissions at Koléa, ENPEI and the École Militaire Polytechnique) take only that year's bac holders aged 18–21. That shuts them to anyone already at university. The MDN's cyber structures do not run public recruitment of their own. The biggest caveat is that the official 2026 recruitment guide on mdn.dz could not be opened during the research. So it is still unconfirmed whether informatique is among this year's sur-titre specialties, whether women can apply in it, what rank you receive and how long you must serve. Check all four on mdn.dz or with a recruitment office before you apply.

## Nine routes on paper, and your age decides which are open

Every route below goes through the MDN's central recruitment system. Since at least 2025, each one starts with **online-only pre-registration on preinscription.mdn.dz** ([MDN recruitment page](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/recrutement/recrutement_fr.php); [Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)). The routes differ in three ways: your status (career officer, contract soldier, civilian employee or conscript), the age ceiling counted at 31 December of the recruitment year, and whether you must be single. The table below sums up the evidence, and the sections after it explain each row and its weak points.

The "Evidence" column uses five labels. **Official text** means the research team read it in the Journal Officiel. **Official extract** means an MDN page seen only through search-engine extracts, because mdn.dz returned HTTP 503 errors throughout the research. **Press** means Algerian media reporting MDN announcements. **Unofficial** means job sites, orientation sites and defence blogs. **Not found** means no source.

Only two routes tolerate an older or married CS graduate: PCA and, for men, national service. National service is an obligation you settle, not a job you choose.

## Military schools train IT officers, but only from fresh bac holders

The ANP trains its own computer scientists from the baccalauréat onward. At the **Académie Militaire de Cherchell**, land-forces officer cadets study for five years: a common military year, then three university years ending in a **Licence académique in Mécanique énergétique, Électronique or Informatique**, then an application year ([MDN Forces Terrestres](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cft/sommaire/recrutement%20et%20formation/recrutement_fr.php); [Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)). Another MDN page calls the same track a "formation d'ingénieur", so the exact degree title is unresolved ([MDN guide 2026](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/recrutement/recrutement_fr.php)).

The **École Supérieure des Transmissions (EST) "Abdelhafidh Boussouf" at Koléa** is the closest thing to a military IT school. Cadets spend a year at Cherchell, then take a three-year Licence that includes a mathematics-and-computer-science track. Masters follow, including one in **information-systems security created in 2018**. A final year specialises in signals, electronic warfare or information and command systems. Graduates are commissioned at the first officer rank (ملازم) ([Radio Algérie, 22 Jul 2019](https://radioalgerie.dz/news/ar/article/20190722/175241.html)). The June 2025 graduation included the 6th "Systèmes d'Information" and 9th "Électronique et Systèmes d'information" Master classes ([MDN, 23 Jun 2025](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/actualites/fr/2025/juin/est23062025fr.php)).

**ENPEI Rouiba** runs a three-year preparatory programme specialising in **Informatique** or engineering sciences, with admission "strictement selon le classement par mérite" ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)). Its first maths-and-computing class graduated in 2022 ([L'Expression](https://www.lexpressiondz.com/info-en-continu/anp-sortie-de-la-21eme-promotion-de-l-enpei-rouiba-331720)). ENPEI feeds the **École Militaire Polytechnique (EMP)**, whose 52nd officer-engineer class in 2025 included informatique ([MDN, 24 Jun 2025](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/actualites/fr/2025/juin/emp24062025fr.php)). Related tracks exist in other forces: a naval Licence in Télécommunications and an air-defence Licence in air control and radiotechnics ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)).

All these schools have the same entry gate. In 2026 they admitted only **holders of the 2026 baccalauréat** with at least **12/20 in Sciences expérimentales, Mathématiques or Technique-Mathématiques**. Candidates had to be **aged 18–21 on 31 December 2026** and single, and had to pass a competition with psychotechnical and physical tests ([MDN guide 2026, extract](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/recrutement/recrutement_fr.php); [Echorouk, 15 Aug 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/military-specializations-attract-high-achieving-baccalaureate-graduates)). A student already at a civilian university fails the current-year-bac condition. The research found no transfer route ("passerelle") that lets a civilian CS undergraduate move into EST, ENPEI or EMP partway through.

The one exception is for people who already hold a Master. EMP's official page lists a **national competition open to Master holders in fields including informatique**, and an on-file route for magistère holders. The dossier is a handwritten request, a CV, a birth-certificate extract, two photos, a copy of the bac, transcripts, copies of the Licence and Master diplomas, and the Master diploma supplement ([MDN, EMP page](https://www.mdn.dz/site_em_anp/sommaire/formation/fr/emp-fr.php)). No dates, quota or age limit are published. The pairing of a Master competition with a magistère route matches the usual structure of Algerian doctoral admissions, so treat this as a postgraduate or doctoral intake whose military or civilian status is unconfirmed. EMP's "master complémentaire" is reserved for serving career officers who already hold an engineer diploma (same source).

In short, the ANP fills its IT officer ranks mainly with people who chose the army at 18. A civilian graduate joins them through the sur-titre intake, competing for whatever informatique posts that year's guide lists.

## The sur-titre officer intake is the main way in for Licence and Master holders

### The rule is 19 plus your years of study, counted at 31 December

The 2026 guide sets four conditions: Algerian nationality, an age of **19 "majorés du nombre d'années minimum requis pour l'obtention" of the diploma**, success in an admission competition, and good physical, mental and psychological condition. Age is assessed at **31 December 2026** ([MDN guide 2026 PDF, extract](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/recrutement/images/guide2026_Fr.pdf)). **You must be single** ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)). The Gendarmerie's official officer page turns the formula into numbers: at most **22 for a Licence académique and 24 for a Master or Ingénieur d'État** ([MDN Gendarmerie, officiers d'active](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cgn/sommaire/form_rec/recrutement/officiers/off_fr.php)). By the same arithmetic, an LMD doctorate (3+2+3 years) would allow about 27, but no source confirms that doctorate holders are recruited this way.

Two more conditions appear only in the press. Awras's summary of the guide adds a **bac average of at least 12/20** ([Awras, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.awras.com/?p=509063)). Echorouk reported that eligible graduates hold **degrees from the 2025–2026 academic year**, which suggests the intake targets new graduates ([Echorouk, 15 Aug 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/military-specializations-attract-high-achieving-baccalaureate-graduates)). Job aggregators give age ranges such as "19–23" or "23 to 25" that contradict the official formula, and you should ignore them ([JOBSdz, Oct 2026](https://jobsdz.com/fr/2026/10/nouvelles-annonces-de-recrutement-au-ministere-de-la-defense-nationale-algerien/)).

Because age is measured on 31 December, only your birth year matters. Take a student born in 2004 who passed the bac in 2022. They finish a Licence in 2025 at 21 and a Master in 2027 at 23, inside both limits with a year to spare. Now take a student born in 2002 who finishes a Master in 2027. They are 25 on 31 December 2027 and are out. In practice, one repeated year still lets you apply with a Master, but two do not.

### Pre-registration is online first, then the competition

The MDN opened electronic pre-registration for élèves officiers de carrière sur titre on **Sunday 2 August 2026**. It ran exclusively through preinscription.mdn.dz, and no physical dossier was accepted at that stage. Each specialty has its own criteria, which are listed in the Guide du recrutement 2026, published around 3 July ([Algerie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/); [Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)). Search results show the sub-path **[preinscription.mdn.dz/fs/](https://preinscription.mdn.dz/fs/)** for this intake, but the page could not be loaded to confirm it.

According to Awras, the initial file is the enquiry form you download after registering online, a copy of your diploma, a copy of your national ID card and a photo ([Awras, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.awras.com/?p=509063)). Applicants could choose several specialisations, and Echorouk reported a strong rush of applicants within 14 days of opening ([Echorouk, 15 Aug 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/military-specializations-attract-high-achieving-baccalaureate-graduates)). Plan on a short window. The closing date, the competition dates and the full later-stage dossier were not found. A job site reported MDN competition results in September 2026 ([JOBSdz, Sept 2026](https://jobsdz.com/fr/2026/09/resultats-des-concours-du-ministere-de-la-defense-nationale/)).

The most recent full paper dossier found for a graduate-officer call dates from 2013. That year the MDN's Direction des Industries Militaires recruited computer-engineering ingénieurs d'État aged at most 24 as career officer cadets. The dossier was: birth certificates of the candidate and his father, a nationality certificate, criminal-record bulletin no. 3, a certified copy of the diploma, a residence certificate, a **certificate of single status**, 16 photos and medical certificates ([dzairjob, citing Echorouk, Aug 2013](https://dzairjob.blogspot.com/2013/08/blog-post.html)). Treat this list as historical, but expect something similar once you pass pre-registration.

### Training lasts about two years, and your starting rank is unconfirmed

The only official training length found is the Gendarmerie's: **two years**. The first is a common basic military year at the Académie Militaire de Cherchell, and the second is specialised professional training ([MDN Gendarmerie](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cgn/sommaire/form_rec/recrutement/officiers/off_fr.php)). That is far shorter than the five-year bac route, but it is still a school phase.

No accessible official text gives the rank you receive at commissioning. A 2023 Arabic defence site says **Licence holders are commissioned ملازم (sous-lieutenant) and Master or Ingénieur holders ملازم أول (lieutenant)**. The same site gives unofficial monthly pay of about **73,000 DZD for a sous-lieutenant and 77,000 DZD for a lieutenant** ([Defense-Arab, 26 Aug 2023](https://defense-arab.com/news/76330/)). Treat both as indicative only.

The minimum length of service, and whether you must repay your training if you leave early, were not found. Career officers fall under the general military statute, Ordonnance 06-02 of 2006, amended by Ordonnance 21-06 of 2021 ([Legal Doctrine](https://legal-doctrine.com/en/edition/le-statut-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral-des-militaires); [Algérie1](https://algerie1.com/de-la-derogation-dage-dans-lexercice-des-hautes-fonctions-de-la-hierarchie-militair)). Read the relevant articles in the Journal Officiel or ask before signing. Becoming an "officier de carrière" is a career decision, not a two-year stint.

### Whether informatique is on the list changes every year

The biggest unknown is the specialty list. MDN force pages state that "la liste des spécialités demandées sera publiée ultérieurement" for each campaign ([MDN Forces Navales](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cfn/sommaire/formation/off_fr.php)). None of the press coverage of the 2026 intake named the specialties ([Algerie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/)). Informatique has been recruited this way before. The 2013 military-industries call explicitly sought computer engineers ([dzairjob, Aug 2013](https://dzairjob.blogspot.com/2013/08/blog-post.html)). The Gendarmerie has also run graduate-officer competitions naming an **ingénieur d'État or Master in informatique** alongside electrical engineering and electronics ([Ennahar, undated](https://www.ennaharonline.com/مسابقـــة-لتوظيف-طلبــــــــــة-آ/)). No source confirms that informatique, networks, cybersecurity or data were on the 2026 list.

### The Gendarmerie and Garde Républicaine recruit through the same intake

The **Gendarmerie Nationale** reports to the MDN and recruits graduate officers through the common sur-titre intake. Its official page accepts an ingénieur d'État, a Master or a Licence académique "in the required specialties", with the 22/24 age limits and two years of training ([MDN Gendarmerie](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cgn/sommaire/form_rec/recrutement/officiers/off_fr.php)). Sources disagree on where the specialised year takes place. One official extract names the judicial police school at Isser (Ysser), while an aggregator says Sétif ([jobs4dz](https://www.jobs4dz.com/%d9%85%d9%84%d9%81-%d9%88%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%a7%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%b5%d9%81%d9%88%d9%81-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d8%b1%d9%83-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b7%d9%86%d9%8a-20/)). In 2024 the Master-holder officer route was open to **men and women**, with minimum heights of **1.70 m and 1.60 m** ([sfnemploi, 18 Jun 2024](https://sfnemploi.com/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B5%D9%81%D9%88%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B1%D9%83-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-2024-%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7/)).

The **Garde Républicaine** is also included in the 2026 sur-titre intake ([Algerie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/)). However, its only documented officer conditions are for the bac route: at least 12/20, five years of training, and minimum heights of **1.78 m for men and 1.65 m for women** ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)). No IT specialty was found, and the height requirement alone rules out most men.

### Contract NCO and rank-and-file posts give your degree no credit

Below officer level, your degree earns you nothing. Contract NCOs (sous-officiers contractuels) need a complete third year of secondary school, with bac holders given priority. They must be aged 18–22 and train for one year (land forces, navy) or two years (air force, Garde Républicaine) ([Echorouk, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%83%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A8)). Contract rank-and-file soldiers (caporal, soldat) need only BEM-level schooling. Their current campaign opened on **23 November 2026** at **preinscription.mdn.dz/hdr/**, covering all forces, the Gendarmerie, the Garde Républicaine and the central services ([Echorouk, 23 Nov 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D9%81%D8%AA%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA)). No source gives a BTS, TS or Licence in informatique a higher starting rank or a reserved IT specialty. No current campaign for contract officers (officiers contractuels) was found either. A Licence holder aged 22 or under who qualifies for both should take the sur-titre officer intake.

## Civilian MDN posts give up rank in exchange for a 35–40 age ceiling

If you are too old for a commission, married, or simply not interested in wearing a uniform, the MDN's **personnels civils assimilés (PCA)** recruitment is your most direct route. It is a civilian competition (concours) run by wilaya, with no military training and no rank.

The **February 2025 campaign** opened on 6 February through preinscription.mdn.dz (sub-path /pca/). It required:

- Algerian nationality;
- **national-service status regularised**;
- a diploma in the specialty;
- **residence in the wilaya of the post**;
- success in the concours.

Candidates uploaded the pre-registration form, a biometric ID card, the diploma, a national-service status certificate, a residence certificate and a photo, and "aucun dossier papier ne sera accepté" ([Algerie360, 6 Feb 2025](https://www.algerie360.com/le-mdn-recrute-des-personnels-civils-assimiles-modalites-et-documents-requis/)). The IT posts were:

- **Ingénieur d'État or Master in informatique**, with options in information systems and maintenance;
- **DEUA in informatique**, with options in networks and informatique de gestion;
- **technicien supérieur in informatique** plus a full third year of secondary school, with a networks option;
- **technicien in informatique**.

A plain Licence in informatique was not on the list ([a-onec, Feb 2025](https://www.a-onec.com/2025/02/mdn-dz-concours.html)).

The **November 2022 campaign** covered force commands, central directorates and military regions. It capped age at 31 December 2023 at **35 for categories 6 and below and 40 for categories 7 and above**. Its informatique posts ran from technicien to Ingénieur d'État, and also included maître assistant and secondary-school teacher (PEM). Candidates used a downloadable form and had 30 days to apply ([L'Express, 10 Nov 2022](https://www.lexpressquotidien.dz/2022/11/10/le-mdn-recrute-des-personnels-civils-assimiles/)). None of the PCA sources mention the single-status rule that applies to military entry.

Three caveats apply. First, campaigns are irregular: November 2022 and February 2025, with no 2026 campaign found, so you cannot plan around a calendar. Second, the sources do not give 2025 age caps, so the 35/40 figures may have changed. Third, the national-service condition blocks a male graduate who has neither served nor been exempted. The research did not establish whether a current student deferral counts as "regularised". Pay grades, and the category an informatique engineer is placed in under the 2023 civilian-personnel statute, were not found.

## National service is compulsory for men at 19 but does not promise IT work

**Loi 14-06 of 9 August 2014** still governs national service. It is **compulsory from 19** and lasts **12 months**. It is served in military form inside the ANP, with no civilian alternative. It starts with registration (recensement) at the commune or consulate in the year you turn 17, between 2 January and 30 September ([JO n°48, 10 Aug 2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).

Several articles matter for students. **Article 28 lets the study deferral (sursis) be renewed until your studies end.** Article 29 refuses it to graduates who enrol at the same or a lower level, so a Licence holder keeps the deferral by starting a Master, but a Master holder starting a second Master loses it. **Article 7 bars anyone whose national-service status is not regular from being hired in the public or private sector.** If you miss the medical selection you are declared fit by default and lose the right to ask for an exemption (Art. 19). Reaching 25 without registering or attending the medical selection makes you insoumis, answerable to a military tribunal (Art. 35) (same source). Consular practice requires you to renew the deferral each academic year with proof of enrolment ([Algerian Embassy Canberra](https://embcanberra.mfa.gov.dz/consular-services/national-military-service)).

The law sorts conscripts into officer cadets (élèves officiers), NCO cadets (élèves sous-officiers) and rank-and-file cadets (élèves hommes du rang). After training it appoints them to one of these grades, "conformément à la réglementation en vigueur": **sous-lieutenant, aspirant, sergent, caporal-chef, caporal or djoundi** (Arts. 44, 47). Promotion during the 12 months happens only for exceptional merit (Art. 72) ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)). Which diploma leads to which grade is set by internal regulation the research could not find. The common belief that graduates serve as officer cadets fits the law but is unconfirmed.

Nothing guarantees IT work. Article 46 allows "formations adaptées" depending on your posting. In 2018 the Direction du Service National drafted a plan to assign conscripts by specialty so graduates gain usable experience, but there is no record that it was implemented ([Echorouk, 1 Apr 2018](https://www.echoroukonline.com/service-national-appeles-orientes-suivant-leurs-specialites)). Article 21 requires you to declare your studies accurately at the medical selection, and doing so is also your best chance of a technical posting. Service counts as professional experience for later recruitment (Art. 70). Afterwards, veterans stay in the reserve for 25 years, with peacetime recalls capped at 30 days a year ([TSA, 11 Aug 2022](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/reserve-militaire-les-categories-de-reservistes-concernees/)).

Two articles link national service to a military career. **Article 60 ends the obligation for anyone recruited into the ANP who serves at least double the legal duration, which is 24 months.** Signing on as a sur-titre officer straight after graduating therefore counts in place of national service rather than adding to it. **Article 71 gives anyone who has completed national service priority to compete for an ANP engagement contract**, but only if they meet the normal conditions, including age ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).

Exemptions come by birth year. In June 2024, men born before 1 January 1995 who had never been called up were freed from the obligation ([Algerie360, 19 Jun 2024](https://www.algerie360.com/service-national-tebboune-donne-la-carte-militaire-aux-jeunes-nes-avant-cette-date/)). In December 2025 a deputy asked for the 1995–1996 cohorts to be covered, and no decision has been found ([ObservAlgérie, 27 Dec 2025](https://observalgerie.com/2025/12/27/societe/service-national-en-algerie-un-depute-demande-a-tebboune-de-regulariser-cette-categorie/)). The law's text covers "tous les citoyens algériens", but in practice only men are called up. An MDN officer has publicly asked deputies to decide whether to include young women ([Ennahar, undated](https://www.ennaharonline.com/لا-معريفة-في-الإعفاء-ولارمي-للفتيات-ب/)).

## Cyber units exist inside the MDN but do not recruit directly

The MDN has built several cyber and IT structures since 2019, and none of them advertises its own recruitment.

- **Agence de sécurité des systèmes d'information (ASSI).** Presidential Decree 20-05 of 20 January 2020 created ASSI as part of a national information-systems-security framework ([ITU strategy repository](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Cybersecurity/Documents/National_Strategies_Repository/nsiss_en.pdf)). It belongs to the MDN and is headed by Brigadier General Abdeslam Belghoul ([Awras, 31 May 2025](https://www.awras.com/?p=435644)). It drafts national strategy, supports state institutions and investigates incidents ([Radio Algérie, 19 Feb 2024](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/40861)). On 3 March 2026 it published the **national information-systems-security strategy for 2025–2029** ([Radio Algérie, 3 Mar 2026](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/80869)), which calls for "compétences et des ressources humaines à l'expérience avérée dans la cybersécurité" ([TSA, 3 Mar 2026](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-deploie-une-strategie-pour-contrer-les-cybermenaces/)).
- **The army's own cyber defence.** A **Service de cyberdéfense et de sécurité des systèmes** oversees a cyber-defence strategy built on seven domains. Its **Centre de cyberdéfense et de surveillance de la sécurité des systèmes** was described in public by its director in 2021 ([Awras, 9 Jun 2021](https://www.awras.com/%d9%85%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%b1-%d9%85%d8%b1%d9%83%d8%b2-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%81%d8%a7%d8%b9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%8a%d8%a8%d8%b1%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d9%8a%d8%b4-%d8%a3%d8%b9%d8%af-%d8%a7/index.md)).
- **Signals and information systems.** The central **Département des Transmissions, des Systèmes d'Information et de la Guerre Électronique** added electronic warfare to its name in July 2019 ([Ennahar, 23 Jul 2019](https://www.ennaharonline.com/الحرب-الإلكترونية-ضمن-اختصاصات-وزارة/)).
- **Gendarmerie cybercrime centre.** The Gendarmerie runs a cybercrime centre (CPLCIC) at Bir Mourad Raïs ([Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/forum-arabe-sur-la-gouvernance-de-linternet-zoom-sur-les-cyberenqueteurs-de-la-gendarmerie/)). Its caseload grew from **909 cases in 2017** ([TSA, 17 Jan 2018](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/cybercriminalite-909-affaires-enregistrees-en-2017/)) to **4,600 in 2022** ([TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/cybercriminalite-en-algerie-les-mises-en-garde-de-la-gendarmerie/)).
- **EPIC-EPMTIC.** In February 2026, presidential decrees created several public enterprises (EPICs) in the ANP's economic sector. One is **EPIC-EPMTIC at El Harrach**, which develops digital and electronic equipment for communication systems. Its board includes the army's central digital directorate and the civilian research centre CDTA ([Algerie360, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/tebboune-dote-le-mdn-de-3-nouvelles-entreprises-voici-leurs-emplacements-et-leurs-missions/)).

The key question is how CS graduates end up in these units. The research found no unit-specific campaign, no MDN-run CTF or hackathon, and no university agreement. Belghoul said ASSI draws on graduates of higher schools specialised in AI and cybersecurity and on vocational-training graduates, without explaining how ([Awras, 31 May 2025](https://www.awras.com/?p=435644)).

The new **École nationale supérieure de cybersécurité** at Sidi Abdellah opened in 2024–2025 as a civilian school under the higher-education ministry ([Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/52492); [Managers.tn, 11 Jun 2024](https://managers.tn/2024/06/11/une-nouvelle-ecole-nationale-superieure-de-cybersecurite-a-alger)). Reports differ on whether the MDN shares its supervision ([Africa Cybersecurity Mag](https://cybersecuritymag.africa/lalgerie-annonce-la-creation-une-ecole-nationale-superieure-de-cybersecurite/); [Horizons](https://www.horizons.dz/?p=166062)). ASSI has run training sessions there for state officials ([APS](https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/actualite-nationale/mpva8cfl-l-assi-organise-la-deuxieme-session-de-formation-au-profit-des-cadres-de-l-etat)), and the school held a doctoral admission competition on 11 July 2026 ([Université de Tiaret notice](https://fmi.univ-tiaret.dz/images/Faculte/2025-2026/Concours%20national%20d.pdf)). The only national cyber hackathon found, in February 2026, was civilian and run by the post and telecommunications ministry ([Radio Algérie, 16 Feb 2026](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/en/node/79924)). How EPIC-EPMTIC staffs itself (officers, contract staff or civilian employees) is not documented.

In practice, you enter through a sur-titre commission, a military school or a PCA post, and then work towards a cyber or IT posting. EST Koléa's Masters in information-systems security and Systèmes d'Information show where the army trains its own cyber officers. The leadership's stated priorities support that ambition. In July 2026 the Chief of Staff, Saïd Chanegriha, told Cherchell graduates that drones, electronic warfare, cyberdefence and AI are "devenus des composantes majeures des champs de bataille modernes" ([TSA, 10 Jul 2026](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/a-cherchell-chanegriha-insiste-sur-la-maitrise-des-drones-de-lia-et-de-la-cyberdefense/)).

## Decision guide: the best move for each situation

| Your situation | Best-fit route | Backup | Do this now |
|---|---|---|---|
| In Licence (L1–L3) | Sur titre with a Licence (≤22) or Master (≤24) | PCA after graduating | Renew your deferral every year; avoid repeating years; read each July's guide |
| Graduating this year | Sur-titre intake in August of your graduation year | Gendarmerie track in the same intake; PCA | Prepare scans by July; pre-register in the first days |
| Past 22 (Licence) or 24 (Master) | PCA | EMP Master competition; national service if still pending | Settle your national-service status; watch for PCA notices |
| Woman | Sur titre or Gendarmerie officer, if the specialty is open to women | PCA | Get written confirmation that the specialty accepts women |
| Already did national service | PCA | Sur titre if still under the age cap; Art. 71 priority for engagement | Keep your carte du service national and certificat de bonne conduite |
| Married | PCA | None found | Every officer and contract military route requires single status |

### Still in Licence: protect your age window and your deferral

The military schools are already closed to you, because they require that year's bac. Two things keep the graduate routes open.

First, protect your age window. Under the 22/24 limits, one repeated year still lets you apply with a Master; two do not.

Second, if you are a man, keep your national-service file clean. Renew your deferral every academic year and never skip a medical-selection call-up (Arts. 19 and 28 of [Loi 14-06](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).

Read the new guide on mdn.dz every July to see whether informatique is on the sur-titre list, and for which forces. If it is, decide whether to apply with your Licence at 22 or wait for your Master at 24. Unofficial sources say the Master brings the higher starting rank. Keep your bac transcript to hand, since the press reported a bac average of at least 12/20 as a condition, and keep fit for the physical tests.

### About to graduate: apply in the August of your graduation year

Your window is the summer you graduate. The guide came out in early July 2026 and pre-registration opened in early August. If that pattern holds, the next window is around **July–August 2027**.

Before July, gather scans of your national ID, your diploma, your bac and transcripts, and photos. Ask whether an attestation de réussite (certificate of success) is accepted before the diploma itself is issued; the research did not establish this. Pre-register in the first days, because applications arrive in a rush.

For men, applying before you are called up for national service is decisive. A Master holder who finishes at 23 and then serves 12 months leaves at 24 or 25, at or past the limit. A commission served for 24 months counts in place of national service. If the Gendarmerie's specialty list includes informatique, apply to that track in the same intake as well. Keep PCA as a backup, remembering that it requires your national-service status to be settled.

### Older graduate: go civilian or postgraduate

If you are past 22 with a Licence or 24 with a Master, every commissioned and contract military route found is closed. Your options are:

- **PCA posts** for Ingénieur d'État or Master, DEUA, TS or technicien in informatique, with caps of 35 or 40 in 2022. Settle your national-service status first, because the next campaign has no fixed date.
- **The EMP competition for Master holders.** Ask EMP directly about its status and age rules before you prepare a dossier.
- **National service**, if you have not yet done it. Declare your diploma and specialty accurately at selection.

Men born before 1 January 1995 who were never called up were exempted in 2024 ([Algerie360, 19 Jun 2024](https://www.algerie360.com/service-national-tebboune-donne-la-carte-militaire-aux-jeunes-nes-avant-cette-date/)).

### Women: voluntary routes only, so confirm your specialty is open

Women are not called up for national service in practice, so you have no conscription route. You also have no national-service status to settle, which should remove the main PCA obstacle that male graduates face. That last point is an inference; check the PCA form's requirements for women.

There is good evidence that women can serve. Women have been ANP officers and NCOs since 1978 ([Carnegie, 2015](https://carnegieendowment.org/fr/posts/2015/11/femmes-dans-le-sanctuaire-des-hommes-le-chemin-vers-legalite-dans-larmee-algerienne)). Military schools have admitted women in telecommunications and engineering specialties ([Afrik.com](https://www.afrik.com/algerie-tebboune-nomme-plusieurs-femmes-generales-une-premiere-historique-dans-le-monde-arabe)), and older reports describe EST Koléa as open to both sexes ([Ennahar, older report](https://www.ennaharonline.com/%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%AA%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%8A-%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7-%D8%A7/)). The Gendarmerie's 2024 officer route for Master holders was open to women, and the Garde Républicaine sets a 1.65 m minimum height for women officers. What is missing is written confirmation that women can apply sur titre in every force and in informatique. Get that answer before you invest in an application.

### Already did national service, or married: PCA is your realistic route

Your carte du service national meets PCA's hardest condition, so PCA is your strongest route. Article 71 gives you priority to compete for an ANP engagement contract, but the age and single-status rules still apply. A Master holder who did national service after graduating is usually just past 24. Keep your certificat de bonne conduite (Art. 57), and count your service as professional experience on applications (Art. 70).

If you are married, every officer and contract military route found requires single status. PCA is the only route that does not mention it.

The binding answers are in three places:

- **The MDN recruitment page and the yearly guide PDF.** The recruitment page is at mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/recrutement, and in 2026 the guide was guide2026_Fr.pdf. These are authoritative on specialties, ages, documents and dates. If mdn.dz is down for you too, the Algerie360 and Echorouk summaries are a fallback, but check them against the PDF.
- **The pre-registration portal, preinscription.mdn.dz.** It shows which campaigns are actually open and which forms they require.
- **The recruitment office of your military region.** The research found no official list of addresses. Ask your local centre du service national or your wilaya's Gendarmerie groupement to direct you, and get answers on women's eligibility, attestation-de-réussite acceptance, starting rank and service commitment in writing.

For the legal texts (Loi 14-06, Ordonnance 06-02 and its 2021 amendment), joradp.dz publishes the Journal Officiel.

## Conclusion

For a CS graduate, getting into the MDN is mainly a timing question, not a matter of finding a unit that wants your skills. No cyber unit takes applications directly, and the sur-titre intake closes about a year after a typical Licence or Master finish. The decision belongs in L2 or M1, not after graduation day. For male students, the order is the hidden lever. If you take a commission first, Article 60 counts it as national service. If you do national service first, the 12 months usually push you past the age cap, leaving PCA as the only door. The same logic makes repeated years more costly than they look on a transcript.

The state's cyber build-up is also mostly civilian. The 2025–2029 strategy is run by an MDN agency, but Decree 26-07 of January 2026 requires a dedicated information-systems-security unit in every public institution ([TSA, 22 Jan 2026](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-se-dote-dun-bouclier-contre-les-cyberattaques/)). For a CS graduate who wants to work on national cyber defence without the age and single-status filters, PCA and civilian state security posts are a real parallel path to the same mission. Whichever route you choose, the most useful thing you can do is read next year's guide the day it is published, because its specialty table answers the question no outside source could: whether informatique is on the list.
