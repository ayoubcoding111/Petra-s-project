# Pay of ANP (Algerian People's National Army) officers by rank, 2024–2026

Research date: 2026-11-26. Scope: Algeria only. Searches were run in Arabic and French. mdn.dz was tried once and returned HTTP 503, so no MDN page was used.

**Rank name note (important for reading Arabic sources):** in Algerian usage, ملازم = sous-lieutenant, ملازم أول = lieutenant, نقيب = capitaine, رائد = commandant, مقدم = lieutenant-colonel, عقيد = colonel. Many Arabic salary sites translate ملازم as "lieutenant". So the "73,000 DZD for a ملازم" figure is the sous-lieutenant figure, and "77,000 for a ملازم أول" is the lieutenant figure. This matches the figures found earlier in this session. (Rank equivalence comes from general Algerian usage, not from a source fetched in this session; it is consistent with how jobsdz.com's French and Arabic versions of the same article pair the ranks.)

**Bottom line on source quality:** I found **no official, published pay scale (grille de solde) or pay amount** for ANP officers. Every DZD figure below comes from unofficial salary or job sites. None cites a decree, a pay slip or the MDN. Treat them as rough orders of magnitude only.

## 1. Legal basis of military pay

### Takeaway
Military pay rests on Ordonnance n° 06-02 of 28 February 2006 (statut général des personnels militaires), articles 52 and following. Pay is a *solde* set by rank and seniority, plus allowances (*indemnités*) linked to function, constraints, posting and risk. I could not find the implementing presidential decree(s) that set the military pay grid or allowance regime. These appear to be separate from the civil-service grid (décret présidentiel 07-304), and they are not readily available online.

### Cited Findings
- Ordonnance n° 06-02 of 28 Feb 2006 is the general statute of military personnel. Under its "articles 52 et suivants", the military person receives a *rémunération* made up of a *solde*, whose amount depends on rank and on seniority in rank and/or in service, plus *indemnités* tied to the nature of the function, its constraints, the place of posting and its risks. A separate military pension and social-security regime applies. The page does not name the decree that fixes the grid. — [Legal Doctrine, "Le statut général des militaires"](https://legal-doctrine.com/edition/le-statut-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral-des-militaires)
- A 2026 presidential decree (signed by Tebboune) sets the conditions for detaching career and contract military personnel to civilian public administrations, under articles 87–88 of Ordonnance 06-02. It says detached military personnel are paid under "the regulations in force within the Ministry of National Defence". This shows military pay follows MDN-specific rules, not the civil-service grid. — [Dzair Tube, 31 July 2026](https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/conditions-for-detachment-of-military-personnel-to-civil-public-administrations-set-forth/) (seen in a search result summary; page not opened)
- For comparison, the **civil-service** grid is set by décret présidentiel n° 07-304 of 29 Sept 2007 (grille indiciaire and pay regime of fonctionnaires). It was amended by décret présidentiel 22-138 of 31 March 2022 (backdated to 1 March 2022, raising minimum indices) and by décret présidentiel 23-54 of 16 Jan 2023 (two stages, January 2023 and January 2024). — [El Hiwar](https://elhiwar.dz/featured/251675/); [El Bilad](https://www.elbilad.net/derniere-info/%D8%B5%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%A6%D8%A7%D8%B3%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AF%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%B8%D9%81%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%88%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9-%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%A8%D9%87%D9%85-103482); [publications.news80.org (text of decree 23-54)](https://publications.news80.org/ar/23-54.html) (all seen in search result summaries)
- A decree setting mission-expense allowances abroad for **both civil and military** personnel exists (décret exécutif). This is one of the few published pay-related texts that names military personnel. — [TSA Algérie](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/frais-de-mission-a-letranger-ce-que-toucheront-les-fonctionnaires-et-les-militaires1) (search result only; not opened)

### Inferences
- Algeria publishes the civil-service grid and its amendments in the Journal officiel. Searches in French and Arabic for a published military grid ("grille indiciaire des personnels militaires", "الشبكة الاستدلالية لرواتب المستخدمين العسكريين") returned only civil-service texts. This suggests the military pay decree(s) are either unpublished or not indexed online. This is an inference from absence, not a confirmed fact.
- Because the *solde* depends on "rank and seniority in rank/service", pay rises with échelons (seniority steps) within each rank, as well as with promotion. The size of each step was not found.

### Gaps
- I could not find the number, date or text of the presidential decree(s) setting the military pay grid or the military régime indemnitaire, or any amendments to them. joradp.dz and mdn.dz were not reachable or not searched directly (mdn.dz returned 503).
- I found no point indiciaire value or index numbers for any military rank.
- I found no échelon table (step size or step frequency) for officers.

## 2. Base salary, allowances and total monthly pay by officer rank

### Takeaway
No official figures exist online. Unofficial sites give two clusters for net monthly pay:
- An older and widely copied set (circa 2022–2023, still recycled in 2025–2026 articles): **sous-lieutenant ~63–73k DZD, lieutenant ~72–77k, capitaine ~79–84k, commandant ~83–89k**.
- A newer September 2025 job-site set: **sous-lieutenant ~90k, lieutenant ~98k, capitaine >115k, commandant 130–150k**.

The second set is more consistent with the 2022–2024 civil-service raises having been passed on, but neither set is verified.

### Inferences
- Gross vs. net: no source separates *solde de base* from allowances, or gross from net. Military pay is generally subject to IRG income tax and social-security deductions, so gross would be higher than these figures. No source quantifies the difference.
- Allowances that sites mention (amounts not given): zone or geographic allowance (South / Grand Sud, which jobsdz says can nearly double base pay, though it says this about the security corps in general, not the ANP specifically), risk allowance, special-forces or unit allowances, and family allowances (spouse and children). Engineers in technical branches may also get a qualification or technical allowance, but no source confirms one for the ANP.
- The older cluster has a gap of only ~4–7k DZD per rank step (ملازم 69/73k, then ملازم أول 72/77k, then نقيب 79/84k). This suggests base pay rises slowly with rank at junior levels. The 2025 jobsdz figures show bigger steps (~8k, then ~17k+).

### Gaps
- I found no breakdown of base solde vs. allowances, and no gross figures.
- No source confirms whether the "~90k sous-lieutenant" (2025) figure includes a zone or other allowance.
- I found no figures for an engineer-officer premium, or for officers who enter with a master's or engineering degree (direct commissioning, *recrutement direct*).

## 3. Raises 2022–2026 and whether they applied to the military

### Takeaway
The 2022, 2023 and 2024 civil-service raises (decrees 22-138 and 23-54) and the raise promised for 2027 are well documented. **I found no published source confirming that these raises were extended to ANP personnel, or by how much.** The jump in unofficial figures from ~73k (2023) to ~90k (2025) for a sous-lieutenant is consistent with a pass-through, but it is not proof.

### Cited Findings
- **2022 (civil service):** Décret présidentiel 22-138 of 31 March 2022 amended the grid (07-304) with effect from 1 March 2022. Its new minimum indices replace the minimum indices in the particular statutes, and staff are reclassified in the new grid while keeping their category and step. — [El Hiwar](https://elhiwar.dz/featured/251675/) (search result summary)
- **2023–2024 (civil service):** Décret présidentiel 23-54 of 16 Jan 2023 amended the grid in two stages (Jan 2023 and Jan 2024). A separate decree amended the grid for contract staff. — [publications.news80.org](https://publications.news80.org/ar/23-54.html); [El Hiwar](https://elhiwar.dz/event/230682/) (search result summaries)
- The 2023 package covered about 2.8 million civil servants and contract staff, cost about 681 billion DZD over 2023–2024, and was implemented by four presidential decrees. — [Radio Algérie (Arabic), Feb 2023](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/21353) (search result summary; the snippet does not mention the army)
- Annual raises of 4,500 to 8,500 DZD depending on rank, from the 2023 plan. — [Echorouk](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%d9%85%d8%ac%d9%84%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b2%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1-%d8%b2%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a8%d9%86%d8%b3%d8%a8%d8%a9-47-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%a6%d8%a9-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%a3/amp) (search result summary)
- **2026–2027 (announced):** In October 2026, President Tebboune repeated his commitment to raise salaries from 2027, in one or two tranches depending on conditions, funded in the next year's budget. Nothing found mentions the military specifically. — [APS](https://www.aps.dz/fr/presidence-news/mut10xq6-le-president-de-la-republique-reitere-son-engagement-a-revaloriser-les-salaires-en-2027); [Algérie 54](https://algerie54.dz/le-president-de-la-republique-reitere-son-engagement-a-revaloriser-les-salaires-en-2027); [La Nouvelle République, 5 Oct 2026](https://www.lnr-dz.com/2026/10/05/revalorisation-des-salaires-a-partir-de-2027/) (search result summaries)
- **Corps-specific revision (Sept 2026):** Tebboune ordered a general pay revision for the Protection civile, through higher point indices, a revised rank grid and higher field allowances. This shows that uniformed corps get separate, statute-specific pay revisions. It is not the ANP. — [Le Matin d'Algérie](https://lematindalgerie.com/protection-civile-tebboune-ordonne-une-revalorisation-des-salaires-et-des-indemnites/); [La Sentinelle, 22 Sept 2026](https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2026/09/22/revalorisation-generale-des-salaires-de-la-protection-civile-les-personnels-affichent-leur-satisfecit/) (search result summaries)
- Searches for "زيادة رواتب أفراد الجيش الوطني الشعبي" and "augmentation salaires personnels militaires ANP" returned no Algerian article confirming a military raise in 2022–2026. Results were dominated by Morocco (Hespress), Libya, Jordan and Canada. — [Hespress (Morocco, not Algeria)](https://www.hespress.com/1394828-1394828.html)

### Inferences
- Unofficial sites' figures rose from ~63–73k (2022–23) to ~90k (Sept 2025) for a sous-lieutenant, about +25–40%. Civil-service raises over 2022–2024 were roughly +4.5k to +8.5k per year depending on category, plus the 2022 index reform. A pass-through to the military of similar size would give roughly the 2025 figures. This is a plausibility check, not evidence.
- If the military follows the civil-service timetable, a further raise would come in 2027. Its size and whether it applies to the military are unknown.

### Gaps
- I found no MDN communiqué, El Djeich magazine article or Journal officiel text confirming military raises in 2022, 2023, 2024 or 2026, or their size.
- I could not confirm whether the military pay grid uses the same *point indiciaire* value as the civil service (45 DZD under 07-304; not re-verified this session).

## 4. Pay of an élève officier (cadet) during training (Cherchell or other military schools)

### Takeaway
**No source with a DZD figure for élève officier pay was found** in either Arabic or French. The only "training pay" figures found concern national-service conscripts (~10,000 DZD in initial training), which is a different status from career cadets.

### Cited Findings
- A search for "منحة الطالب الضابط شرشال" / "راتب الطالب الضابط" returned only pages on graduated officers' pay and rank structure. None gave a cadet stipend. — e.g. [city-salary.com ranks page](https://city-salary.com/%D8%B1%D8%AA%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B4-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1%D9%8A/); [jobs4dz.com ranks by education 2025](https://www.jobs4dz.com/%D8%B1%D8%AA%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B4-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B9%D8%A8%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%AD%D8%B3%D8%A8-%D8%A7/) (search results)
- Conscript (service national) figures: ~10,000 DZD during initial training, ~25,000 DZD as a conscript ملازم after graduation. These are not career-cadet figures. — [unlimited-news.com](https://www.unlimited-news.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%AA%D8%A8-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AC/) (search result summary)
- The legal-doctrine summary of Ordonnance 06-02's pay articles says nothing about élèves. — [Legal Doctrine](https://legal-doctrine.com/edition/le-statut-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral-des-militaires)

### Inferences
- Élèves officiers are normally fed, housed and equipped by the school, and receive a modest monthly allowance (*prêt*) far below a sous-lieutenant's solde. This is general background, not a sourced Algerian figure; the amount should be treated as unknown.
- For a graduate engineer entering by direct recruitment, the training period may be short and may be paid at the rank of aspirant or sous-lieutenant. Neither the rank on entry nor its pay was confirmed in sources found this session.

### Gaps
- I found no figure for élève officier or aspirant pay, from any source quality level.
- I found no MDN recruitment notice (avis de recrutement) stating training pay. mdn.dz was unreachable (HTTP 503).

### Inferences
- The earlier session figure (~73k sous-lieutenant / ~77k lieutenant, defense-arab.com, 2023) is **confirmed as a widely circulated unofficial number**, but it is **not verified** and is probably out of date by 2025–2026. The best available unofficial update is jobsdz's September 2025 figures (~90k / ~98k net).
- For a new engineer-officer in 2025–2026, a defensible unofficial estimate is **~75–95k DZD/month net for a sous-lieutenant, rising to ~80–100k as lieutenant and ~100–120k as capitaine**, with large extra amounts possible from a southern posting or special allowances. A further raise is possible in 2027 if the announced public-sector revalorisation is extended to the military.
