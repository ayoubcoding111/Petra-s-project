# Extra pay for engineers and cyber/IT specialists in the Algerian military (ANP/MDN), and pay of civilian engineers at the MDN (PCA), 2024–2026

Scope note: research date 26 Nov 2026. mdn.dz was tried once (connection reset, no response) and not used. The Journal Officiel (joradp.dz) worked, and the primary decrees below were read directly from it (or from the gazettes.africa mirror for JO 2023 n° 22). "Via search summary" marks a figure that comes from a search-engine summary rather than a page I read in full.

## Q1. Do military engineers (EMP ingénieurs d'État or officers recruited "sur titre") get specific allowances (qualification, technicité, spécialité, sujétion, risque, rendement, signals/EW/cyber)? How much more than a regular officer of the same rank?

### Takeaway
The legal basis for such allowances exists. Ordonnance 06-02, art. 52, says military pay is a solde (set by rank and seniority) plus indemnités tied to the nature of the function, its special constraints, the posting location and its risks. However, **no published decree, official grid or credible report gives the ANP's régime indemnitaire, or any engineer, technicité or cyber allowance amount or rate.** No source quantifies how much more an engineer or cyber specialist earns than a regular officer of the same rank. Unofficial websites put a lieutenant at roughly 77,000–98,000 DA/month (2023–2025) and a "military engineer" at 70,000–140,000 DA. None of them cites an official source.

### Cited Findings
**Official legal frame (primary texts, read directly)**
- Ordonnance n° 06-02 of 28 Feb 2006 (statut général des personnels militaires), **art. 52**: "Le militaire a droit à une rémunération composée d'une solde dont le montant est fixé en fonction du grade, de l'ancienneté dans le grade et/ou dans le service et d'indemnités liées à la nature de la fonction exercée et des sujétions particulières qu'elle impose, au lieu d'affectation et aux risques qui lui sont rattachés. Il peut bénéficier de prestations en nature fixées par voie réglementaire." — [JO n° 12, 1 Mar 2006 (joradp.dz)](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2006/F2006012.pdf)
- Same article: "Toute mesure de portée générale affectant la rémunération des fonctionnaires de l'Etat est, sous réserve des adaptations nécessaires, étendue aux personnels militaires." — [JO n° 12, 2006](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2006/F2006012.pdf)
- Art. 53: the solde cannot be transferred or seized. Military personnel in special leave keep "l'intégralité de leur solde et des indemnités en rapport avec leur grade et leur dernière fonction". — [JO n° 12, 2006](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2006/F2006012.pdf)
- Art. 110: officers are recruited only by competition through officer-cadet schools. Conditions on age, titles and diplomas are set by presidential decree. Statuts particuliers set "les grades initiaux pour chaque corps et les modalités de prise de rang". — [JO n° 12, 2006](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2006/F2006012.pdf)
- Searches in French and Arabic for a decree on the military régime indemnitaire ("régime indemnitaire personnels militaires", "منحة التأهيل العسكريين", "النظام التعويضي للمستخدمين العسكريين") found **no published text**. Results were either French (Légifrance) military-allowance decrees or Algerian military-pension texts. — [search results, e.g. Ennahar on 2021 military pensions code](https://www.ennaharonline.com/%D8%B5%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B3%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7/)
- One possible source of confusion: an "indemnité de qualification" at 40% of traitement for categories ≤ 12, published in JO n° 04 of 23 Jan 2025, belongs to **décret exécutif 25-55, the régime indemnitaire of the education sector's specific corps**. It is **not** a military or MDN allowance. — [JO n° 04, 23 Jan 2025 (joradp.dz)](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2025/F2025004.pdf)

**Unofficial figures (aggregator and blog sites; no official source cited; treat as rough estimates)**

Sergent about 68,000 DA; contract soldier 48,000 DA, rising to about 65,000 DA in the South or elite units. It says entry pay is "particulièrement compétitif ... surtout pour les spécialités techniques et rares" but gives **no engineer premium amount**. - City-Salary (31 Jan 2025), "military engineer" monthly pay: overall 70,000–140,000 DA; lieutenant from about 70,000 DA; major/colonel above 120,000 DA. By specialty: **telecommunications 85,000–135,000**, electrical 80,000–130,000, mechanical 75,000–125,000, civil 70,000–120,000, aviation up to 140,000. Informatique and cyber are not listed. It names allowances (housing, transport, risk, field missions, travel, duty, experience) **without amounts**. No official source. — [City-Salary, Jan 2025](https://city-salary.com/%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%87%D9%86%D8%AF%D8%B3-%D8%B9%D8%B3%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1/)

It also says Master or Ingénieur holders can be recruited directly as lieutenant (ملازم أول). — [Defense-Arab, Aug 2023](https://defense-arab.com/news/76330/) (as recorded in prior session notes)
- Other aggregators: Profuti (2024) lieutenant 69,000 DA; hmsalaries lieutenant about 73,000 DA. - Context: EMP graduates in informatique and telecom tracks (including a Master in "information-systems security", created 2018) are commissioned as lieutenant after a final year of specialization in signals, EW or command information systems. — [Radio Algérie, 22 Jul 2019](https://radioalgerie.dz/news/ar/article/20190722/175241.html) (from prior session notes)

### Inferences
- Because art. 52 extends general civil-service pay measures to the military, the 2022–2024 civil-service index increases (+50 points in 2022, then the 23-54 grids of 2023 and 2024; see Q3) were probably passed on to the solde in some adapted form. No text confirms the military amounts.
- The structure (solde + function, sujétion, location and risk indemnities) makes a technical-specialty indemnity for engineers or signals/EW/cyber staff plausible. In the civilian public service, engineers get a 40% "indemnité des services techniques communs" against 25% for technicians (Q3), so a differential of that order is a reasonable **hypothesis only**. It is not evidence about the ANP.
- On the unofficial figures, the engineer "premium" over a same-rank officer cannot be separated out. City-Salary's telecom range (85–135k) is about 15k above its generic engineer floor (70k), but this looks like editorial guesswork.
- The unofficial lieutenant figures rose from about 69–77k DA (2023–2024) to about 98k DA (2025). This fits the extension of the 2023–2024 civil-service increases, but the sites' methods are unknown.

### Gaps
- No official ANP solde grid or régime indemnitaire was found. The relevant decrees and MDN arrêtés appear to be unpublished, and mdn.dz was unreachable.
- No source gives an amount or rate for an indemnité de qualification, technicité, spécialité, sujétion, risque or rendement for military engineers, or any signals/EW/cyber allowance.
- Whether an "ingénieur d'État sur titre" enters as lieutenant or sous-lieutenant is confirmed only by unofficial sources.
- I found no credible report (forum or press) by a serving officer that compares an engineer's pay with a non-engineer's pay at the same rank.

## Q2. Is there a special pay regime for cyber/information-systems security personnel (military or civilian) at the ASSI or the army cyber-defense service? Any decree on its régime indemnitaire?

### Takeaway
No. Décret présidentiel 20-05 (20 Jan 2020) makes the ASSI a **public administrative establishment (EPA) of the MDN**. It sets no salary or allowance regime. It gives the agency's comité d'orientation the power to deliberate "sur les rémunérations des personnels de l'agence", and art. 39 subjects the agency to **all MDN regulations**. No later decree on an ASSI régime indemnitaire, or on pay in the army's cyber-defense service, was found.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 17: "L'agence est un établissement public à caractère administratif, dotée de la personnalité morale et de l'autonomie financière", with its seat in Algiers. — [JO n° 04, 26 Jan 2020, Décret présidentiel 20-05 (joradp.dz)](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf)
- Art. 20: the agency has a comité d'orientation, a comité scientifique, a director general, a **"centre national opérationnel de sécurité des systèmes d'information"**, and technical and administrative directorates and services. — [JO n° 04, 2020](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf)
- Art. 24: the comité d'orientation deliberates on "les plans de recrutement et de formation des personnels ainsi que sur les rémunérations des personnels de l'agence". — [JO n° 04, 2020](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf)
- Art. 21, 27 and 29: the chair of the comité d'orientation, the DG, the directors, the head of the national operational centre and the heads of service are all appointed "conformément à la réglementation en vigueur au sein du ministère de la défense nationale". The DG has hierarchical authority over all staff and handles the "habilitation" (security-clearance) procedure for staff (art. 28). — [JO n° 04, 2020](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf)
- Art. 35–36: the budget comes from state subsidies. It is prepared by the DG, deliberated by the comité d'orientation and approved by the Minister of National Defence. Art. 39: "L'agence est soumise à l'ensemble des dispositions législatives et réglementaires applicables au sein du ministère de la défense nationale." Art. 40: national-defence information systems are excluded from the decree's scope. — [JO n° 04, 2020](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf)
- A search for an ASSI régime indemnitaire found only the 20-05 text itself and unrelated French decrees. — [search, LinkedIn summary of 20-05](https://fr.linkedin.com/pulse/bref-aper%C3%A7u-sur-le-nouveau-decret-presidentiel-20-05-portant-aradji)
- Decree 23-143 (2023) on MDN civilian staff covers civilians working in "établissements militaires à caractère administratif" and in MDN central and regional structures (art. 3 and 6). Civilian ASSI staff therefore fall under it, as PCA or contract civilians (see Q3). — [JO n° 22, 4 Apr 2023](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2023/dz-government-gazette-dated-2023-04-04-no-22.pdf)

### Inferences
- ASSI staff are paid under **MDN rules**: military staff under the military solde and indemnities, civilian staff under the 23-143 PCA or contract framework. A dedicated ASSI pay scale, if it exists, would be an internal MDN decision approved through the comité d'orientation and the Minister. It is not published.
- I found no evidence of a cyber-specific allowance like the cyber retention bonuses in some Western militaries.

### Gaps
- No text found on pay or allowances in the army's "Service de cyberdéfense et de sécurité des systèmes" or its monitoring centre.
- The MDN internal arrêté on ASSI organisation (art. 30 and 34) and any staff-pay deliberation were not found.

## Q3. PCA civilian engineers at the MDN: which category/grade does an Ingénieur d'État / Master in informatique get, what is the monthly pay in 2025–2026, and is there a PCA régime indemnitaire decree (e.g., 23-143)?

### Takeaway
**Décret présidentiel 23-143 of 27 March 2023 (JO n° 22, 4 Apr 2023) is verified.** It is the statute of civilian personnel of the MDN, both PCA and civilian contract staff. It does **not** contain a pay grid or régime indemnitaire. It extends every civil-service pay measure to MDN civilians and leaves the grids, primes and indemnités to "réglementation en vigueur au sein du MDN", which is unpublished. In the civil service, an **ingénieur d'État en informatique is catégorie 13**. Under the latest official grid (in force since **1 Jan 2024**, point at 45 DA), that gives a **base traitement of 778 × 45 = 35,010 DA/month** at échelon 0. Adding the corps-communs allowances for engineers (40% technical indemnity, plus a 0–30% prime de rendement) gives an illustrative gross of about **49,000–59,500 DA/month**. **No grid change has been published for 2025 or 2026.** The only 2026 pay measure found is the SNMG rise to 24,000 DA.

### Cited Findings
**Decree 23-143 (PCA statute), read directly**
- Title: "Décret présidentiel n° 23-143 du 5 Ramadhan 1444 correspondant au 27 mars 2023 portant statut des personnels civils relevant du ministère de la défense nationale". It sets up two cadres: "personnels civils assimilés aux personnels militaires" (permanent) and "personnels civils contractuels" (fixed-term or open-ended contracts). It cites the 1974 decree n° 74-60 that created the PCA cadre. — [JO n° 22, 4 Apr 2023 (gazettes.africa mirror)](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2023/dz-government-gazette-dated-2023-04-04-no-22.pdf)
- Art. 5: "Toutes mesures et dispositions affectant la rémunération, l'organisation et le déroulement de carrière des fonctionnaires et des agents de l'Etat, sont étendues au profit des personnels civils du ministère de la défense nationale, sous réserve des adaptations nécessaires". — [JO n° 22, 2023](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2023/dz-government-gazette-dated-2023-04-04-no-22.pdf)
- Art. 8: grades are classified by filière and corps. Their classification "s'inspire de la législation et de la réglementation nationales en vigueur avec les adaptations liées aux spécificités d'emploi au sein du MDN". Art. 9: four groups, **"A"** (conception, recherche, études: engineer level), "B" (application), "C" (maîtrise), "D" (exécution, contract only). Art. 12: the nomenclature of grades and posts is set by **arrêté of the Minister of National Defence**. — [JO n° 22, 2023](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2023/dz-government-gazette-dated-2023-04-04-no-22.pdf)
- Art. 28: "une rémunération comprenant le traitement, les primes et les indemnités, conformément à la réglementation en vigueur au sein du MDN", plus "allocations familiales" and possible "prestations en nature". Art. 33: groups are subdivided into categories by qualification level, and "en raison du haut niveau de qualification exigé, certains grades appartenant au groupe 'A', peuvent être classés dans des subdivisions hors catégories". Art. 34: "Les grilles indiciaires des traitements applicables aux personnels civils ... sont fixés conformément à la réglementation en vigueur au sein du MDN." — [JO n° 22, 2023](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2023/dz-government-gazette-dated-2023-04-04-no-22.pdf)
- Other relevant articles: art. 6 (PCA may serve anywhere, including abroad under the rules for military personnel abroad); art. 14 (nationality, regular national-service status, age ≥ 18, aptitude); art. 15 (administrative security investigation); art. 23 (free care in military health facilities for PCA, retirees and dependants); art. 37 (overtime capped at 20% of legal hours for contract staff); art. 42 (2.5 days of leave per month, maximum 30). — [JO n° 22, 2023](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2023/dz-government-gazette-dated-2023-04-04-no-22.pdf)

**Category of the informatique engineer (civil-service reference)**
- Décret exécutif 08-04 (corps communs), filière "Informatique", classification table: **Ingénieur d'application cat. 11 (grade being phased out); Ingénieur d'État cat. 13; Ingénieur principal cat. 14; Ingénieur en chef cat. 16**; Technicien supérieur cat. 10; Technicien cat. 8. The original 2008 minimum indices were 498 / 578 / 621 / 713 / 453 / 379. — [JO n° 03, 20 Jan 2008 (joradp.dz)](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2008/F2008003.pdf)
- Art. 107: ingénieurs d'État en informatique are recruited by competition from holders of "un diplôme d'ingénieur d'État en informatique ou d'un titre reconnu équivalent". Art. 109: ingénieur principal is recruited from holders of a **magister** in informatique. The 2008 text does not mention the Master. — [JO n° 03, 2008](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2008/F2008003.pdf)
- The Feb 2025 MDN PCA campaign listed "Ingénieur d'État/Master informatique (systèmes d'information; maintenance)" as a single post type. — [a-onec, Feb 2025](https://www.a-onec.com/2025/02/mdn-dz-concours.html) (from prior session notes); [Algerie360, 6 Feb 2025](https://www.algerie360.com/le-mdn-recrute-des-personnels-civils-assimiles-modalites-et-documents-requis/)

**Grid and point value (official)**
- Décret 07-304, art. 5: traitement = (minimum index of the category + échelon index) × point value. Art. 8: "La valeur du point indiciaire ... est fixée à quarante-cinq (45) DA." Art. 10–11: 12 échelons, one step every 2.5–3.5 years. Art. 16: prior experience is credited at **1.4% of base pay per year** in public administrations and 0.7% per year elsewhere. Art. 7: "Les indemnités rémunèrent des sujétions particulières ... ainsi qu'au lieu et aux conditions spécifiques de travail. La prime rémunère le rendement et la performance." — [JO n° 61, 30 Sep 2007 (joradp.dz)](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2007/F2007061.pdf)
- Décret présidentiel **22-138** (2022) added 50 index points across the grid. Cat. 13 rose from 578 to **628**, so base pay went from 26,010 to 28,260 DA. — [Décret 22-138, JO n° 23, 6 Apr 2022 (univ-bejaia copy)](https://univ-bejaia.dz/doc/personnel/Decret-presidentiel-22-138.pdf) (via search summary); [taa3lim worked example](https://taa3lim.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AF%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AC%D9%88%D8%B1-2024-%D9%88%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9/) (via search summary)
- Décret présidentiel **23-54 of 16 Jan 2023** (JO n° 03, 17 Jan 2023) amends 07-304 with two grids. **From 1 Jan 2023:** cat. 10 = 578, cat. 11 = 623, cat. 12 = 662, **cat. 13 = 703**, cat. 14 = 746, cat. 16 = 838. **From 1 Jan 2024:** cat. 10 = 653, cat. 11 = 698, cat. 12 = 737, **cat. 13 = 778**, cat. 14 = 821, cat. 16 = 913, cat. 17 = 962. Cat. 13 échelon increments in 2024: 1st +39, 2nd +78, 3rd +117 ... 12th +467. Art. 3: the new minimum index replaces the one in the statuts particuliers. — [JO n° 03, 17 Jan 2023 (joradp.dz)](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2023/F2023003.pdf)
- Base traitement from the 2024 grid × 45 DA (my arithmetic on the official indices): **cat. 13 = 35,010 DA** (échelon 0), 36,765 DA (échelon 1), 56,025 DA (échelon 12). Cat. 14 = 36,945 DA; cat. 16 = 41,085 DA; cat. 10 (TS) = 29,385 DA; cat. 11 = 31,410 DA. This matches the CarrierePlus-DZ simulator, which shows a base of **35,010.00 DA** for several cat.-13 ingénieur d'État grades. — [JO n° 03, 2023](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2023/F2023003.pdf); [CarrierePlus-DZ, grade 410](https://dzcarriereplus.com/grade/410/); [CarrierePlus-DZ, grade 182](https://dzcarriereplus.com/grade/182/)
- Cat.-13 base pay history: 26,010 DA (2008–2021) → 28,260 (2022) → 31,635 (2023) → **35,010 (2024 to date)**, which is +34.6% since 2021 (my arithmetic). — sources as above

**Allowances that apply to civil-service informatique engineers (reference for PCA)**
- The régime indemnitaire of the corps communs (three decrees of 13 May 2010, effective 1 Jan 2008) includes: a **prime de rendement of 0–30%** of traitement, paid quarterly after rating; and an **"indemnité des services techniques communs"** for the "laboratoire et maintenance" and "informatique et statistiques" filières, paid monthly at **40% of traitement for engineers** and 25% for techniciens, adjoints and agents techniques. — [Algerie360, 1 Jun 2010](https://www.algerie360.com/fonction-publique-trois-nouveaux-regimes-indemnitaires-promulgues/)
- The CarrierePlus-DZ simulator gives an "initial net" of **45,910 DA** (agriculture and statistics cat.-13 engineers) and **48,235 DA** (public-works cat.-13 engineer) on the 35,010 DA base. It also estimates a quarterly prime de rendement of about 25,806 DA. These are unofficial simulator outputs, and the method is not shown. — [CarrierePlus-DZ, grade 722](https://dzcarriereplus.com/grade/722/); [grade 410](https://dzcarriereplus.com/grade/410/)
- An old forum post (unofficial, before the 2022–2024 increases) put a civilian ingénieur d'État en informatique at an APC in Algiers at about 35,000 DA/month plus a quarterly bonus of about 20,000 DA. — [ForumDZ thread](https://www.forumdz.com/topic/39765-salaire-dun-ing%C3%A9nieur-d%C3%A9tat-en-informatique-%C3%A0-lapc-de-b%C3%A9b%C3%A9-elle-oued-alger-svp/) (via search summary)

**2025–2026 updates**
- On 30 Nov 2025 the Council of Ministers raised the **SNMG from 20,000 to 24,000 DA from January 2026** (Décret présidentiel 26-01 of 7 Jan 2026, JO n° 01/2026, via search summary). The APS report mentions **no change** to the civil-service grid or the point value. — [APS, 30 Nov 2025](https://www.aps.dz/fr/presidence-news/mim6ow3o-)
- Online claims of a "53% increase" in 2026–2027, or a point-value rise, are **speculative scenarios** that the sites themselves say depend on future official texts. — [Unlimited-News](https://www.unlimited-news.com/%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%88%D8%B1-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-2026-%D8%AD%D9%82%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82/); [Elmofid](https://elmofid.com/20262027-2/) (unofficial)
- In Sept 2026, Tebboune ordered a pay revision (revised index plus a field allowance) for **Civil Protection** agents, a civilian uniformed corps. No similar ANP or MDN-civilian measure was found. — [Dzair Tube](https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/tebboune-orders-pay-rise-for-algerian-civil-protection-personnel) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- **Illustrative monthly gross for a new PCA ingénieur d'État/Master en informatique, 2025–2026**, assuming the MDN copies the civil-service classification (art. 8) and the corps-communs allowances (art. 5): traitement 35,010 + 40% technical indemnity 14,004 = **49,014 DA**, plus prime de rendement 0–10,503 DA/month (paid as up to about 31,500 DA per quarter). That is **about 49,000–59,500 DA gross/month** before social-security and IRG deductions. Net is probably about 45,000–55,000 DA, which fits the simulator's 45.9–48.2k "initial net". After 12 échelons (30–42 years), the traitement reaches 56,025 DA. This is my calculation; the MDN's actual PCA indemnities are unpublished.
- Art. 33 of 23-143 means an engineer-level PCA grade could be placed **hors catégorie** inside the MDN. That would mean higher pay than cat. 13, but no evidence shows this is done for informatique.
- Comparison: on unofficial figures, a lieutenant (about 77–98k DA, 2023–2025) earns roughly **1.5–2× a starting PCA ingénieur d'État** (about 49–60k gross). A PCA post trades the pay gap for civilian status: no celibacy rule, no military training, and free military health care (art. 23).
- The Master is treated as equivalent to the ingénieur d'État for PCA recruitment (2025 campaign wording), so a Master holder most likely enters at cat. 13 as well. No MDN arrêté confirms this.

### Gaps
- The MDN arrêté setting the PCA grade nomenclature (23-143 art. 12) and the PCA grids and régime indemnitaire (art. 28, 34) were not found. They are probably unpublished internal regulations.
- No official PCA payslip or MDN-published figure was found.
- The exact IRG and social-security deductions for 2025–2026 were not sourced, so net figures are approximate.
- No published text treats the Master as equivalent to ingénieur d'État under 08-04. Amendments to 08-04 were not checked.

## Q4. Location (Sud / isolated zones), housing, family allowances and danger pay for these profiles

### Takeaway
All four exist in principle for the military (Ord. 06-02, art. 52: location, risk, prestations en nature) and for MDN civilians (23-143: allocations familiales, prestations en nature, extra leave in the Sud). **No amounts specific to the MDN were found.** The national family allowances are small and old: 600 DA per child (children 1 to 5, for incomes ≤ 15,000 DA) and 300 DA from the 6th child. The civil-service Southern zone allowance is still calculated on a 1989 base salary, which unions disputed in 2022. Unofficial claims say Sud postings can greatly increase military pay.

### Cited Findings
- **Military:** indemnities are linked to "lieu d'affectation" and "risques", and "prestations en nature" are set by regulation. — [Ord. 06-02, art. 52, JO n° 12, 2006](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2006/F2006012.pdf)
- **PCA/MDN civilians:** "Outre la rémunération, ils perçoivent des allocations familiales" and may receive "prestations en nature" (art. 28). Staff working in the **wilayas du Sud**, or abroad in certain zones, get a **bonification du congé** (extra leave) set by arrêté of the Minister of National Defence (art. 45). — [JO n° 22, 2023](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2023/dz-government-gazette-dated-2023-04-04-no-22.pdf)
- **Sud zone allowance (civil service):** in 2022, civil servants in 11 southern wilayas went on strike to demand that the zone allowance be updated and calculated on current pay instead of the **1989 base salary**. — [Maroc Diplomatique, 2022](https://maroc-diplomatique.net/fonction-publique-greve-generale-et-rassemblements/) (via search summary)
- **Sud (military, unofficial):** JOBSdz says Sud and border postings bring "substantial" compensation and that the extreme-South zone allowance can "presque doubler" base pay in some cases. Its contract-soldier example rises from 48,000 to about 65,000 DA in the South or elite units (about +35%). No source is cited. — [JOBSdz, Sep 2025](https://jobsdz.com/fr/2025/09/salaires-en-algerie-police-gendarmerie-armee-grille-complete-2025/)
- **Family allowances (national scheme):** 600 DA per child for the 1st to 5th child, and 300 DA from the 6th, where the contributory wage is ≤ 15,000 DA. The schooling allowance is 800 DA per child. The single-wage allowance is 4,800 DA per year (since 1 Jan 2004). — [Algerie360, allocations familiales](https://www.algerie360.com/allocations-familiales-algerie-conditions-cnas/) (via search summary). The labour minister said the child and schooling allowances have **not been raised since 1997**. — [Echaab](https://www.echaab.dz/?p=143406) (via search summary)
- **Housing and danger pay:** City-Salary lists housing, risk and field-mission allowances for military engineers but gives **no amounts**. JOBSdz lists generic "prime de risque", "indemnité de responsabilité" and family allowances, but none specifically for the ANP. — [City-Salary, Jan 2025](https://city-salary.com/%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%87%D9%86%D8%AF%D8%B3-%D8%B9%D8%B3%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1/); [JOBSdz, Sep 2025](https://jobsdz.com/fr/2025/09/salaires-en-algerie-police-gendarmerie-armee-grille-complete-2025/)
- Some civilian engineer corps have a statutory "تعويض الخطر" (risk indemnity), for example cat.-13 agriculture engineers under décret exécutif 08-286. No such indemnity is listed for the informatique corps-communs grade. — [CarrierePlus-DZ, grade 182](https://dzcarriereplus.com/grade/182/)

### Inferences
- For an engineer on more than 15,000 DA a month, which covers every case here, family allowances are worth at most a few hundred to a few thousand DA a month. They do not change the overall picture much.
- The Sud allowance is the most likely large add-on for military personnel, especially in the extreme South. For civil servants and probably PCA, it is capped in practice by the 1989 calculation base. Both points come from unofficial or indirect sources.
- Housing for officers is probably provided in kind (art. 52 "prestations en nature"). No rules on logement de fonction or its cash value were found.

### Gaps
- No official ANP rates for the Sud/zone allowance, risk allowance, or housing allowance or logement de fonction.
- The current civil-service rates for the Southern zone allowance by wilaya, and whether PCA receive them, were not found.
- Family-allowance amounts for incomes above 15,000 DA were not confirmed in the sources read.
