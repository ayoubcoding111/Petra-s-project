# Cyber defense, IT and digital units of the Algerian military (ANP / MDN) open to CS graduates, and how they recruit

Research date: 2026-11-25. Sources are mostly Algerian state media (APS / Radio Algérie), Algerian press (TSA, Algérie360, Awras, Ennahar, Echorouk) and a few aggregators. mdn.dz returned HTTP 503 when fetched, so I could not read the official 2026 recruitment guide directly. "Verified" below means a fetched article said it explicitly. Search-snippet-only claims are flagged.

---

## 1. What cyber / IT / signals structures exist in the ANP–MDN? (names, creation dates)

### Takeaway
At least four structures are confirmed: (a) the **Agence de sécurité des systèmes d'information (ASSI)**, created by Presidential Decree 20-05 (20 Jan 2020) and part of the MDN; (b) the army's own **Service de cyberdéfense et de sécurité des systèmes** with its **Centre de cyberdéfense et de surveillance de la sécurité des systèmes** (publicly described in 2021); (c) the central MDN **Département des Transmissions, des Systèmes d'Information et de la Guerre Électronique** (renamed to add EW in July 2019); and (d) the Gendarmerie's **cybercrime centre** at Bir Mourad Raïs. I found no source for a formal "Commandement de la cyberdéfense" (a French-style COMCYBER). The army structure is described as a "service" (مصلحة), not a command.

### Cited Findings

**A. ASSI: Agence de sécurité des systèmes d'information (وكالة أمن الأنظمة المعلوماتية), MDN**
- Presidential Decree n° 20-05 of 20 January 2020 set up a "dispositif national de sécurité des systèmes d'information" made of a **National Council** and an **Agence de sécurité des systèmes d'information (ASSI)**. — [ITU National Strategies Repository (nsiss_en.pdf)](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Cybersecurity/Documents/National_Strategies_Repository/nsiss_en.pdf)
- Awras (31 May 2025) says the agency was created by Decree 20-05 of 20 Jan 2020, belongs to the Ministry of National Defence, and works in permanent coordination with all state institutions. — [Awras, 31 May 2025](https://www.awras.com/?p=435644)
- Its Director General is **Brigadier General (العميد) Abdeslam Belghoul**. On 31 May 2025 he received the APN National Defence Committee, chaired by Bourhane Ben Guerina, at the agency's HQ. This was described as the first visit of its kind. — [Awras, 31 May 2025](https://www.awras.com/?p=435644)
- Missions Belghoul described in Feb 2024 (forum on digital sovereignty, University of Algiers 3): drafting the national information-systems-security strategy, supporting state institutions in securing their IS, and **responding to cyber incidents through investigation, assistance and periodic awareness-raising**. — [Radio Algérie (AR), 19 Feb 2024](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/40861)
- On **3 March 2026**, the "Agence de sécurité des systèmes d'information du MDN" published the **Stratégie nationale de sécurité des systèmes d'information 2025-2029**, approved by President Tebboune "Président de la République, Chef suprême des forces armées, Ministre de la Défense nationale". Its main axes are posted on mdn.dz. — [Radio Algérie (AR), 3 Mar 2026](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/80869); [WeAreTech Africa, 5 Mar 2026](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/cybersecurite-l-algerie-adopte-sa-strategie-nationale-2025-2029)
- Strategy axes reported by TSA: data protection and sovereignty; early detection and anticipation of attacks; four years of support for public and private bodies; cyber-resilience; sensitive sectors; legal and normative framework; national and international cooperation. TSA says the strategy requires mobilising "compétences et des ressources humaines à l'expérience avérée dans la cybersécurité". — [TSA, 3 Mar 2026](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-deploie-une-strategie-pour-contrer-les-cybermenaces/)
- Under 20-05, private operators had to designate an information-systems security officer before 26 Jan 2021. — [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/25386-entry-into-force-with-grace-period-presidential-decree-no-20-05-on-establishing-a-national-framework-for-the-security-of-information-systems-including-cybersecurity-regulation)
- **Naming discrepancy:** TSA calls it the "Agence *nationale* de sécurité des systèmes d'information". APS/Radio Algérie and Awras use "Agence de sécurité des systèmes d'information" (no "nationale"). — [TSA, 3 Mar 2026](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-deploie-une-strategie-pour-contrer-les-cybermenaces/) vs [Radio Algérie, 3 Mar 2026](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/80869)
- **Unverified:** a search-result summary of Echaab/Awras coverage said the National Council is chaired by the **Minister of National Defence or his representative**. I could not confirm this in a fetched article or in the JO text. — [Awras ?p=25874 (search result, not fetched)](https://www.awras.com/?p=25874)

**Related civilian-side decree (creates demand for cyber staff across the state, but these are not MDN posts)**
- Presidential Decree **n° 26-07 of 7 Jan 2026** (JO of 21 Jan 2026) requires every public institution, administration and body to have a **dedicated IS-security structure**. It must be independent of the IT department, report to the top official, and operate "permanente et en toute circonstance". Its missions include the IS security policy, risk maps, audits and controls, continuous monitoring, immediate incident reporting, and personal-data compliance, all "in line with the national strategy". — [TSA, 22 Jan 2026](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-se-dote-dun-bouclier-contre-les-cyberattaques/)

**B. Army cyber defense service and centre (internal ANP)**
- In a 2021 interview with **El Djeich** magazine, the director of the **"مركز الدفاع السيبراني ومراقبة أمن الأنظمة"** (Centre de cyberdéfense et de surveillance de la sécurité des systèmes), **Lieutenant-Colonel (المقدم) Nabti Mokrem**, said the ANP has a general cyber-defense strategy built on **7 domains**. All specialized ANP bodies contributed to it, and the **"مصلحة الدفاع السيبراني وأمن الأنظمة"** (Service de cyberdéfense et de sécurité des systèmes) supervises its implementation. The 7 domains are not listed. — [Awras, 9 Jun 2021](https://www.awras.com/%d9%85%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%b1-%d9%85%d8%b1%d9%83%d8%b2-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%81%d8%a7%d8%b9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%8a%d8%a8%d8%b1%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d9%8a%d8%b4-%d8%a3%d8%b9%d8%af-%d8%a7/index.md)
- **Search-snippet only:** the commander of the École de Commandement et d'État-Major is reported as saying the service helps train officer trainees in repelling external cyberattacks. — [search-result summary of Awras/Echorouk coverage; the Echorouk piece is titled "الدفاع السيبراني طرف فعال في العمليات"](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%81%d8%a7%d8%b9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%8a%d8%a8%d8%b1%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%8a-%d8%b7%d8%b1%d9%81-%d9%81%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%84-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b9%d9%85%d9%84%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%aa/amp)

**C. Département des Transmissions, des Systèmes d'Information et de la Guerre Électronique (دائرة الإشارة وأنظمة المعلومات والحرب الإلكترونية)**
- In July 2019 a presidential decree (JO n° 42) renamed the central MDN department "دائرة الإشارة وأنظمة المعلومات" (Transmissions et Systèmes d'Information) to "دائرة الإشارة وأنظمة المعلومات والحرب الإلكترونية", **adding electronic warfare**. Major General Abdelkader Lechtchem was named its head. — [Ennahar, 23 Jul 2019](https://www.ennaharonline.com/الحرب-الإلكترونية-ضمن-اختصاصات-وزارة/)
- In Aug 2021 Tebboune appointed Farid Bedjghit head of the department. Bedjghit died on 2 Nov 2021, and **Brigadier General Youcef Nabil Titouch** was installed by MDN Secretary General Maj. Gen. Mohamed Salah Benbicha. — [Awras, 13 Nov 2021](https://www.awras.com/%d8%aa%d9%86%d8%b5%d9%8a%d8%a8-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b9%d9%85%d9%8a%d8%af-%d9%8a%d9%88%d8%b3%d9%81-%d9%86%d8%a8%d9%8a%d9%84-%d8%aa%d9%8a%d8%aa%d9%88%d8%b4-%d8%b1%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%b3%d8%a7-%d9%84%d8%af%d8%a7/)
- Missions per an unofficial recruitment-info site, which uses a different name ("دائرة الإشارة ومنظومات القيادة والسيطرة"): managing and securing military telecom networks, operating information and command systems, maintaining electronic equipment, and ensuring secure communications between units. **Treat with caution.** — [sfnemploi.com](https://sfnemploi.com/%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D9%88%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A/)
- Former Chief of Staff Ahmed Gaïd Salah inaugurated a **Centre national des transmissions et de la guerre électronique** (المركز الوطني للإشارة والحرب الإلكترونية). The date is not given in the result and is presumably before 2019. — [Awras (search result)](https://www.awras.com/?p=10901)
- Signals-equipment unit: in Feb 2022 Chanegriha visited the **"المؤسسة المركزية لتجديد عتاد الإشارة"** (Établissement central de rénovation du matériel des transmissions) at **El Harrach**, 1st Military Region. — [Awras, 28 Feb 2022](https://www.awras.com/%d8%b4%d9%86%d9%82%d8%b1%d9%8a%d8%ad%d8%a9-%d9%8a%d8%b4%d8%af%d8%af-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d8%b9%d8%b5%d8%b1%d9%86%d8%a9-%d9%85%d9%83%d9%88%d9%86%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7/index.md)
- A "central digital directorate of the army" (direction centrale du numérique) sits on the board of the new ICT EPIC (see Q2). — [Algérie360, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/tebboune-dote-le-mdn-de-3-nouvelles-entreprises-voici-leurs-emplacements-et-leurs-missions/)

**D. Gendarmerie nationale (part of the ANP/MDN)**
- The Gendarmerie runs a **Centre de prévention et de lutte contre la criminalité informatique et la cybercriminalité** at **Bir Mourad Raïs** (Algiers), presented at the Arab Internet Governance Forum. The article date was not visible. — [Algérie360](https://www.algerie360.com/forum-arabe-sur-la-gouvernance-de-linternet-zoom-sur-les-cyberenqueteurs-de-la-gendarmerie/)
- The MDN's 2026 recruitment guide covers ANP training establishments, including the **Gendarmerie nationale** and **Garde républicaine**. — [Algérie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)

### Inferences
- In the MDN the cyber function appears split between three bodies: (1) a **national, cross-government regulator and responder (ASSI)**; (2) an **internal army cyber-defense service and monitoring centre (SOC-like)**; and (3) the **signals/IS/EW department**, which owns military networks and EW. The Gendarmerie adds a **law-enforcement cyber-forensics** branch.
- The name "Centre de cyberdéfense et de **surveillance** de la sécurité des systèmes" suggests a security-operations / monitoring function. This is my reading of the name, not a stated mission.
- A "Direction centrale de lutte contre la cybercriminalité", whose new HQ was inaugurated in Algiers in Oct 2023, appears in search results ([Ambassade d'Algérie à Zagreb / APS](https://www.ambalgzagreb.com/?p=8795)). Nothing I fetched attaches it to the MDN, and it is most likely a DGSN (police, Ministry of Interior) structure. Do not count it as a military unit without checking.

### Gaps
- No official org chart: I could not confirm where the "Service de cyberdéfense et de sécurité des systèmes" sits (État-Major? the signals/IS/EW department? the security services?), its creation date or decree, or its current head.
- No source on cyber units of the military security / intelligence services (DCSA, DGSI, DDSE). Nothing found.
- Current (2026) head of the signals/IS/EW department not found. Titouch was the last confirmed head (Nov 2021).
- The JO text of Decree 20-05 was not fetched, so the Council's chair and composition are unverified.
- The "direction centrale du numérique" is named only in passing in one Algérie360 article. I found no other source on its creation or mandate.

## 2. Military R&D and defense-industry entities that hire CS engineers: military or civilian (PCA) posts?

### Takeaway
The clearest new entry point is the **EPIC "Moyens des TIC" (EPIC-EPMTIC)**, created in Feb 2026 under MDN tutelle at El Harrach to develop digital and electronic equipment for communication systems. Signals-equipment maintenance units also exist. I found no source that says whether engineers in these entities are recruited as officers, contract military personnel, or civilians (PCA / EPIC employees).

### Cited Findings
- Three presidential decrees of **17 Feb 2026** created three EPICs attached to the **ANP economic sector**, under MDN tutelle and based on Decree 08-102 (2008) on the ANP economic sector. They are **EPIC-EC** (câblerie / optical fibre, Réghaïa), **EPIC-EPMTIC** (ICT, El Harrach) and **EPIC-EDIT** (technical textiles). They were published in JO n° 16 (early March 2026), and each is headed by a general or senior officer. Decree numbers reported in search-result summaries: 26-104, 26-105 and 26-106, not checked against the JO. — [Algérie360, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/tebboune-dote-le-mdn-de-3-nouvelles-entreprises-voici-leurs-emplacements-et-leurs-missions/); [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-etend-son-industrie-militaire-a-trois-secteurs-cles/); [APA](https://fr.apanews.net/security/algerie-emprise-de-larmee-sur-trois-secteurs-industriels/); [Observ'Algérie, 5 Mar 2026](https://observalgerie.com/2026/03/05/economie/larmee-algerienne-se-lance-dans-trois-nouveaux-secteurs-industriels/)
- **EPIC-EPMTIC ("Moyens des TIC")** is meant to develop "équipements numériques et électroniques pour les systèmes de communication". It is intended to link the military and scientific research, turning lab innovations into operational tools for public institutions. Its board includes the army's **central digital directorate** and the **Centre de Développement des Technologies Avancées (CDTA)**, a civilian research centre. It has legal personality and financial autonomy. — [Algérie360, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/tebboune-dote-le-mdn-de-3-nouvelles-entreprises-voici-leurs-emplacements-et-leurs-missions/)
- In Feb 2022 Chanegriha, visiting the Établissement central de rénovation du matériel des transmissions (El Harrach), called for "a solid industrial base in communications of all kinds" and continuous improvement of communication systems. — [Awras, 28 Feb 2022](https://www.awras.com/%d8%b4%d9%86%d9%82%d8%b1%d9%8a%d8%ad%d8%a9-%d9%8a%d8%b4%d8%af%d8%af-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d8%b9%d8%b5%d8%b1%d9%86%d8%a9-%d9%85%d9%83%d9%88%d9%86%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7/index.md)
- 21 ANP production units took part in a military-industry / subcontracting fair. This comes from a search-result summary; the date was not confirmed. — [Radio Algérie (FR) node/63501](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/63501)
- **Not MDN:** ENIE (Entreprise Nationale des Industries Électroniques, Sidi Bel Abbès) is a civilian public company with 1,100+ staff "dont des ingénieurs et des techniciens". In June 2026 it announced 8 research agreements, including with Université Djillali-Liabès, covering "systèmes intelligents et numérisation". None of the sources I found establishes a link to the MDN. — [Algérie-Eco, 6 Jun 2026](https://algerie-eco.com/2026/06/06/enie-renforce-son-activite-industrielle-grace-a-8-accords-de-recherche-strategiques/)

### Inferences
- EPIC-EPMTIC and the other ANP-economic-sector EPICs are the most likely places where a CS engineer could work on defense-related digital and electronic products. EPICs normally employ civilian staff under labour-law contracts alongside seconded officers, but **none of the sources confirms how EPIC-EPMTIC staffs itself.**
- With the CDTA on EPIC-EPMTIC's board, civilian-research profiles (embedded systems, microelectronics, software) are likely to be relevant. This is my inference from the board composition.

### Gaps
- No source found on a dedicated ANP "Centre de recherche et de développement" in IT/cyber, on Direction des Fabrications Militaires hiring of CS engineers, or on R&D labs at EMP.
- No source found on **PCA (personnel civil assimilé)** recruitment of IT engineers by the MDN. A search hit for an MDN civilian-posts notice ([El Bilad](https://www.elbilad.net/s@itfol7ar110194)) did not visibly include computer engineering. Not fetched; unverified.
- ASAL / Centre de Développement des Satellites links were not researched in depth (time budget). No finding.

## 3. Cybersecurity competitions, hackathons, CTFs or talent programs run by the MDN or military schools

### Takeaway
I found **no evidence** of a public CTF, hackathon or talent-scouting program run by the MDN, ASSI, EMP or any other military school. The only national-level cyber hackathon I found (Feb 2026) was civilian, run by the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications.

### Cited Findings
- The Ministry of Post and Telecommunications ran the **first sectoral cyber-attack simulation exercise and cyber hackathon**, closing in Feb 2026. It included phishing, ransomware and DoS scenarios, and Minister Sid Ali Zerrouki praised students of the **École nationale supérieure des TIC et de la Poste (ENSTICP)**. The article mentions no MDN/ASSI involvement and no recruitment link. — [Radio Algérie (EN), 16 Feb 2026](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/en/node/79924)
- Belghoul (ASSI) said in May 2025 that the national strategy includes awareness campaigns "showcasing national expertise". No competition was mentioned. — [Awras, 31 May 2025](https://www.awras.com/?p=435644)
- Searches in French ("MDN hackathon cybersécurité École militaire polytechnique CTF") returned only French and Canadian military CTFs (COMCYBER "Passe Ton Hack d'Abord", Canadian DND CTF). None were Algerian. — [search: e.g. Le Monde Informatique on COMCYBER contest](https://www.lemondeinformatique.fr/actualites/lire-comcyber-reorganise-un-concours-de-hack-etudiant-dans-toute-la-france-95748.htm)

### Inferences
- The MDN does not appear to use public competitions as a visible recruitment pipeline, unlike the French COMCYBER. Entry is through formal recruitment campaigns (Q7). Absence from open-web sources does not prove absence: such events might exist internally or appear only on mdn.dz or Facebook pages that I could not reach.

### Gaps
- mdn.dz news archives and military-school social media were not searchable or fetchable. A school-level CTF club at EMP or at a signals school may exist but is undocumented in my results.

## 4. MDN–civilian university partnerships; is there a new national cybersecurity school, and does it have a military link?

### Takeaway
Yes. The **École nationale supérieure de cybersécurité** opened in **2024-2025** at the Sidi Abdellah university pole (Algiers). It is a **civilian school under the Ministry of Higher Education (MESRS)**. The MDN is said to have been a collaborator at the project stage (Sept 2023, single secondary source), and ASSI's head cites it as part of the national cyber build-up. ASSI says it draws on graduates of specialised higher schools in AI and cybersecurity. I found no formal MDN-university convention that leads to military careers.

### Cited Findings
- **Sept 2023:** the Council of Ministers examined a project to create a national cybersecurity school. A search-result summary reports it was to be led by the **MESRS "in collaboration with the Ministry of National Defence"**. I could not see the original communiqué; the claim comes from aggregator coverage. — [WeAreTech Africa brief](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-une/l-algerie-creera-une-ecole-nationale-de-cybersecurite); [Osiris](https://osiris.sn/Algerie-vers-la-creation-d-une.html)
- **June 2024:** the school is announced at Sidi Abdellah, Algiers, under **MESRS** tutelle by presidential decree. — [Managers.tn, 11 Jun 2024](https://managers.tn/2024/06/11/une-nouvelle-ecole-nationale-superieure-de-cybersecurite-a-alger); [Horizons (AR), Jun 2024](https://www.horizons.dz/language/ar/2024/06/%D9%86%D8%AD%D9%88-%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%B3%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84/)
- **2 Jul 2024:** Abdelhakim Djebrani, MESRS finance director, said the creation decree was already published and the school would open at the next rentrée "conformément aux instructions du président de la République". — [Radio Algérie (FR), 2 Jul 2024](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/48057)
- **2024-2025 rentrée:** Minister Kamel Baddari opened the academic year at the École nationale supérieure de cybersécurité, located in the Abdelhafid-Ihaddadene sciences and technology campus, Sidi Abdellah. — [Radio Algérie (FR) node/52492](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/52492)
- **2026:** a doctoral admission competition at the school was scheduled for **11 July 2026** (Sidi Abdellah university pole notice). — [Université de Tiaret notice (PDF)](https://fmi.univ-tiaret.dz/images/Faculte/2025-2026/Concours%20national%20d.pdf); [Université de M'Sila](https://www.univ-msila.dz/site/national-competition-for-admission-to-doctoral-programs-in-the-national-higher-schools-affiliated-with-the-sidi-abdellah-university-center/)
- In Feb 2024, ASSI DG Belghoul listed the decision to create a "national higher school of cybersecurity" among the presidential measures that give cybersecurity high priority. — [Radio Algérie (AR), 19 Feb 2024](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/40861)
- In May 2025, Belghoul said ASSI draws on **graduates of higher schools specialised in AI and cybersecurity**, and that **vocational-training graduates** are also a "vital source" of personnel. — [Awras, 31 May 2025](https://www.awras.com/?p=435644)
- **EMP (École Militaire Polytechnique, Bordj El Bahri)** is an MDN higher-education institution "under the pedagogical supervision of the MESRS". Its programs were being aligned with the LMD system. — [Ennahar, 17 Jun 2013](https://www.ennaharonline.com/%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%AC-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%80-40-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%87%D9%86%D8%AF%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A8/)
- Civilian–military research link: the Feb 2026 ICT EPIC under the MDN has the civilian **CDTA** on its board and aims to "link the military and scientific research". — [Algérie360, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/tebboune-dote-le-mdn-de-3-nouvelles-entreprises-voici-leurs-emplacements-et-leurs-missions/)
- Belghoul's Feb 2024 speech was given at a **University of Algiers 3** (Faculty of Political Science) forum on digital sovereignty. This shows ASSI engages academically, but it is not a formal convention. — [Radio Algérie (AR), 19 Feb 2024](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/40861)

### Inferences
- Graduates of the École nationale supérieure de cybersécurité are an obvious talent pool for ASSI, as Belghoul says, but the school is civilian. Joining the MDN from it would still go through MDN recruitment (officer "sur titre", or a civilian/contract post), not a direct pipeline. **No source confirms a reserved quota or a convention.**
- The first cohort entered in 2024-25. If the cycle is 5 years (typical for Algerian écoles supérieures; not confirmed), the first engineers would graduate around 2029. The 2026 doctoral intake suggests a parallel postgraduate track.

### Gaps
- No text found of any MDN–MESRS or MDN–university convention (ESI, USTHB, ENSIA, ENSI) on cyber/IT training or doctoral programs that lead to military careers.

## 5. Official statements (2020–2026) on cyber, digitisation and specialist needs

### Takeaway
The Chief of Staff, Gen. Saïd Chanegriha, repeatedly lists cyber defense, AI, EW, drones and data as core to modern warfare (2021, 2022, 2024, April 2026, July 2026). ASSI and the 2025-2029 strategy explicitly call for proven cyber human resources. I found **no explicit public call** by the MDN leadership to "recruit young IT graduates"; the statements are about modernisation and training.

## 6. What roles and specialties do CS profiles fill?

### Takeaway
Officially stated functions map onto: secure military networks and command information systems (signals/IS/EW department); SOC-type monitoring and cyber defense (army cyber centre); incident investigation, audit, strategy and support to institutions (ASSI); EW; cyber-forensics (Gendarmerie); and digital/electronic equipment for communications (EPIC-EPMTIC). On the training side, EMP has an **informatique** branch, ENPEI offers an **informatique** specialisation, and naval officers can take a **Licence in Télécommunications**.

### Cited Findings
- ASSI functions: national IS-security strategy, support to institutions, **incident response via investigation, assistance and awareness**. — [Radio Algérie (AR), 19 Feb 2024](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/40861)
- Functions required in every public body by decree 26-07 (civilian demand driven by the ASSI strategy): IS security policy, risk mapping, security / data-protection / cyber-resilience solutions, audits, continuous monitoring, incident reporting. — [TSA, 22 Jan 2026](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-se-dote-dun-bouclier-contre-les-cyberattaques/)

## 7. How these structures recruit CS profiles (entry routes)

### Takeaway
The documented routes are: (1) **bac route** into MDN schools (ENPEI informatique, EMP, signals school, navy telecom licence); (2) **"élèves officiers de carrière sur titre"** for university graduates, with online pre-registration on **preinscription.mdn.dz** (2026 campaign opened 2 Aug 2026; age limit 19 + required years of study; single); (3) contract NCOs. I found **no unit-specific recruitment** (ASSI or the cyber service hiring directly) and no PCA IT campaign.

### Cited Findings
- The MDN published the **Guide du recrutement 2026** on mdn.dz in early July 2026. It covers diplomas, age criteria and documents for all ANP training establishments, including the Gendarmerie and Garde républicaine. — [Algérie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/); [MDN Guide de recrutement 2026 page (503 at fetch time)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/recrutement/recrutement_fr.php)
- **Officers "sur titre" (university graduates):** online pre-registration opened **2 Aug 2026**, exclusively via **preinscription.mdn.dz** (no paper files). Age limit is **19 years plus the number of years of study required for the targeted specialty**, and **celibacy is mandatory**. Each filière has its own criteria, and the required diplomas are listed in the Guide. — [Algérie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/); [Echorouk (EN), 15 Aug 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/military-specializations-attract-high-achieving-baccalaureate-graduates)
- **Bac route (2026):** bac 2026 with at least 12/20 ("Assez Bien"), age 18-21 at 31 Dec 2026, competitive exam with psychotechnical and physical tests. Contract NCOs: 3rd year of secondary school completed (priority to bacheliers), age 18-22. — [Algérie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)
- Applicants may apply to several specialisations, and more than 50 are offered. — [Echorouk (EN), 15 Aug 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/military-specializations-attract-high-achieving-baccalaureate-graduates)
- ASSI's stated talent sources are graduates of higher schools specialised in AI and cybersecurity, plus vocational-training graduates. No recruitment drive or university agreement is mentioned. — [Awras, 31 May 2025](https://www.awras.com/?p=435644)

### Inferences
- For a CS graduate (licence / master / ingénieur), the most plausible military route is **"officier de carrière sur titre"** in an informatics / telecom / IS filière. Assignment to ASSI, the cyber service or the signals/IS/EW department then follows internal posting; no source shows candidates choosing a cyber unit directly. The celibacy requirement and the age cap are important filters.
- Because recruitment runs through a single MDN campaign (July-August each year), timing matters: the Guide appears in early July and "sur titre" pre-registration opens in early August (2026 pattern).
- Civilian-status options (EPIC-EPMTIC employee, PCA) may exist but are undocumented in my sources.

### Gaps
- The exact list of informatics diplomas and filières accepted "sur titre" in 2026 is in the Guide, which was not retrievable (mdn.dz 503).
- No information on selection tests specific to IT profiles, security clearance procedures, or contract length / mandatory service years for "sur titre" officers.
- No source on whether foreign-trained or dual-national graduates are eligible.
