## Q1. Current legal framework: Loi 14-06, duration, ages, sursis, exemptions, regularisation

### Takeaway
Loi n° 14-06 of 9 Aug 2014 is still the governing text. It sets 12 months of continuous service inside the ANP, registration (recensement) in the year of turning 17, and call-up at 19. A study deferral (sursis) can be renewed until studies end, but it is refused to graduates who enrol in a programme at the same or a lower level. Anyone without a regular service status cannot be hired in the public or private sector. Since 2014, exemptions by birth cohort have been granted by communiqué or decree, not by amending the law. The latest found is the June 2024 exemption for men born before 1 Jan 1995 who were never incorporated. In Dec 2025 deputies asked for the 1995-1996 cohorts to be covered, and no decision on that was found.

### Cited Findings
**Law text, JO n° 48 of 10 Aug 2014 [OFFICIAL]** ([JO n°48/2014 PDF](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)):
- Art. 1: national service is "la participation des citoyens à la défense nationale" and is "destiné à répondre aux besoins de l'Armée Nationale Populaire".
- Art. 3: service is compulsory "pour tous les citoyens algériens âgés de dix-neuf (19) ans révolus".
- Art. 4: service is performed "en la forme militaire au sein des structures de l'Armée Nationale Populaire" and "de façon continue durant toute sa durée légale". There is no civilian alternative.
- Art. 5: "La durée légale du service national est fixée à douze (12) mois."
- Art. 7: "Tout citoyen ne justifiant pas de sa situation régulière vis-à-vis du service national ne peut être recruté dans le secteur public ou privé, ou exercer une profession ou une activité libérale." This directly limits CS graduates' civilian employability until their status is settled.
- Art. 9: insoumis and national-service soldiers fall under military tribunals.
- Arts. 10-13: recensement covers all citizens in Algeria or abroad who turn 17 during the current year. They register on the tables of the commune of residence or at a consulate and receive an "attestation de recensement". Registration runs 2 Jan to 30 Sep each year.
- Arts. 16, 19: attendance at the medical selection is compulsory. Anyone who ignores the convocation, outside force majeure, is "déclarés aptes d'office et perdent le droit à la demande de dispense".
- Art. 21: at the service national structure, citizens must declare their level of studies accurately, along with address changes. A false declaration made to avoid incorporation costs the right to dispense and to report d'incorporation.
- Art. 22: the request for dispense, report d'incorporation or sursis pour études ou formation is filed at the medical selection.
- Arts. 24-26: a dispense for a "cas social digne d'intérêt" can be granted "même après l'incorporation". It is decided by a regional commission in each military region, with appeal to the central service national structure at the MDN.
- Art. 27: a report d'incorporation (postponement) is possible for a justified temporary impediment, or when a brother is serving under the flag.
- Art. 28: the sursis for studies or training "peut être renouvelé jusqu'à achèvement des études ou de la formation". The law sets no age ceiling.
- Art. 29: the sursis "ne peut être accordé aux citoyens diplômés s'étant inscrits à des cursus de même niveau ou d'un niveau inférieur". A CS licence holder can keep a sursis by enrolling in a master. A master holder who enrols in a second master cannot.
- Art. 30: the supervising ministries send the MDN's central service national structure yearly lists of enrolled students and of graduates.
- Arts. 31-34: call-up at 19. Call-up orders are handed over directly or through the Gendarmerie nationale. Refusing to sign the acknowledgement still counts as notification. A no-show gets a second order for the next contingent.
- Arts. 35-36: a person is insoumis if he fails to join after a call-up, or if he reaches 25 without completing recensement or medical selection. A complaint is then filed before the territorial military tribunal.
- Art. 60: obligations end for those who served the legal duration, those declared unfit, those exempted as social cases, and those "recrutés dans les rangs de l'ANP, et ayant servi durant une période au moins égale au double de la durée légale", which means 24 months.
- Art. 61: also freed are those "déclarés aptes non incorporables" by the MDN's central structure. This is the "fit but not incorporated" status.
- Art. 75: the law repeals Ordonnance 74-103 (the old Code du service national) and Loi 89-19, which had reduced the duration.

**Duration history [PRESS]:** the 2014 law cut service from 18 to 12 months, after an earlier cut from 24 to 18 months. It applied from the October 2014 contingent. Algerie360 also notes that time served counts toward pension ([Algerie360, 9 Sep 2014](https://www.algerie360.com/la-nouvelle-loi-sur-le-service-militaire-publiee-dans-le-journal-officiel/)).

**Implementing texts:** the MDN lists an arrêté of 16 Sep 2014 on applying the law to soldiers already serving at publication (MDN "lois" page, seen only as a search snippet because the site was unreachable: [mdn.dz lois_fr](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/service/lois_fr.php)). No amendment to Loi 14-06 up to Nov 2026 turned up in these searches.

**Exemptions and regularisation measures:**
- June 2024 [OFFICIAL via PRESS]: the MDN announced that young citizens born before 1 Jan 1995 who had not been incorporated were freed from their obligations. They had to report to the "centres de service national" on a schedule set by birth month (30 June to mid-Sept 2024), bringing a copy of the national ID card and 2 photos. No decree number is cited ([Algerie360, 19 Jun 2024](https://www.algerie360.com/service-national-tebboune-donne-la-carte-militaire-aux-jeunes-nes-avant-cette-date/)).
- Presidential Decree n° 24-184 of 11 June 2024 set exemption measures for citizens abroad, also covering those born before 1 Jan 1995 who had not been called up. A separate "presidential exemption" covers people born 2 Nov 1959 to 31 Dec 1983 ([Algerian Embassy Canberra](https://embcanberra.mfa.gov.dz/consular-services/national-military-service)) [OFFICIAL].
- A 2020 Tebboune decree exempting citizens aged 30 and over is mentioned only in a search-engine summary of the Algerie360/ObservAlgérie articles. It was not verified on a primary page, so treat it as unconfirmed.
- Dec 2025: an APN deputy (Arfis Marouane) asked President Tebboune and the Prime Minister for a decree exempting men born in 1995 and 1996 who were never incorporated. Many of them hold university degrees and are blocked from recruitment competitions ([ObservAlgérie, 27 Dec 2025](https://observalgerie.com/2025/12/27/societe/service-national-en-algerie-un-depute-demande-a-tebboune-de-regulariser-cette-categorie/); [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/service-militaire-un-depute-saisit-le-1er-ministre-pour-la-regularisation-de-ces-deux-classes/)) [PRESS]. No adopted measure was found.
- Recent call-ups: an MDN notice told young men born 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2006 that they must attend the medical selection under Loi 14-06 (search snippet of [Algerie360, "Le ministère de la Défense publie une annonce importante"](https://www.algerie360.com/service-national-le-ministere-de-la-defense-nationale-publie-une-annonce-importante/)) [PRESS]. The embassy page lists the class of 2028 (born 2008) as being registered from 2 Jan to 30 Sep 2025 ([Embassy Canberra](https://embcanberra.mfa.gov.dz/consular-services/national-military-service)).
- Dual nationals (France): under the 11 Oct 1983 Franco-Algerian accord they may serve in either country. A 2026 guide says the option must be exercised before age 25 ([Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/service-national-dispense-sursis-algeriens-etranger/); [ZoomAlgérie](https://zoomalgerie.com/service-militaire-algerien-binational-france/)) [PRESS].

### Inferences
- A CS student can stay deferred through licence, then master, then doctorate, because each step is a higher level. The sursis ends at the last diploma, or as soon as a graduate re-enrols at the same or a lower level (Art. 29). Most CS graduates therefore face call-up at about 22-25 years old, right after the licence or master.
- Art. 60's "served at least double the legal duration" clause means a young man who signs directly as a career or contract soldier in the ANP, including the Gendarmerie and Garde Républicaine, and serves 24 months has met his national service obligation. Direct military recruitment is therefore a substitute for national service, not something added on top of it.
- Exemptions in practice come in batches tied to birth cohorts (pre-1995 in 2024). Men born in 1995 and later should not expect a cohort amnesty unless a new decree is issued.

### Gaps
- No consolidated or amended version of Loi 14-06 was found. mdn.dz and joradp.dz could not be read from this environment, so it was not possible to confirm that no amendment exists as of 2026.
- The implementing décrets and arrêtés (training duration under Art. 45, monthly allowance amounts under Art. 62, the list of "situations vis-à-vis du service national" under Art. 7) were not retrieved.
- The decree number and date of the 2020 "30 and over" exemption could not be verified.

## Q2. What happens to university graduates (CS) during service: rank, use of specialty, training

### Takeaway
The law sorts conscripts into élèves officiers, élèves sous-officiers and élèves hommes du rang. After military training they are appointed to a grade on a fixed list: sous-lieutenant, aspirant, sergent, caporal-chef, caporal or djoundi. The grade is set "conformément à la réglementation en vigueur", and the published law does not tie a grade to a diploma. The ANP can give conscripts training adapted to their posting, and the Direction du Service National stated in 2018 that it intends to assign conscripts according to their specialty so graduates gain usable experience. No official source found guarantees that a CS graduate is posted to IT duties.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 44: national-service soldiers are classed as "élèves officiers du service national; élèves sous-officiers du service national; élèves hommes du rang du service national" ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)) [OFFICIAL].
- Art. 45: conscripts receive "une formation militaire dont la durée et le contenu sont fixés par arrêté du ministre de la défense nationale". The law sets no length for basic training ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).
- Art. 46: "Outre la formation militaire et le métier des armes, les militaires du service national peuvent bénéficier de formations adaptées selon leurs lieux d'affectation" ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).
- Art. 47: after training, conscripts are appointed, "conformément à la réglementation en vigueur", to one of: sous-lieutenant, aspirant, sergent, caporal-chef, caporal, djoundi ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).
- Art. 72: during service, promotion happens only for exceptional merit (a feat of arms or act of bravery) or posthumously. The grade received after training is effectively the grade held for the whole 12 months ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).
- Art. 62: each conscript receives a monthly allowance ("allocation mensuelle") fixed per grade by regulation ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).
- Art. 70: time in national service counts toward seniority for promotion and retirement, "Il est considéré comme une période d'expérience professionnelle pour le recrutement" ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).
- Arts. 67-69: incorporation suspends any employment relationship. The employer must reinstate the person at the same or an equivalent post, "même en surnombre", within at most 6 months of release, with all acquired rights ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).
- Apr 2018 [PRESS]: the Direction du Service National (DSN) at the MDN prepared an "étude exhaustive" to assign conscripts to roles matching their specialties, training and preferences. The goal was to let university and vocational graduates gain experience they can use after service. A final draft was sent to the then chief of staff, Gen. Gaïd Salah. The article does not mention informatique and does not report whether the plan was implemented ([Echorouk FR, 1 Apr 2018](https://www.echoroukonline.com/service-national-appeles-orientes-suivant-leurs-specialites)).
- 1974 [OFFICIAL, historical]: the 1974 Code du service national (now repealed) described a two-year service in which all conscripts received military training before posting (search summary of [JO 1974 n°99](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/1974/dz-government-gazette-dated-1974-12-10-no-99.pdf)). This is context only and was repealed by Art. 75 of Loi 14-06.

### Inferences
- The 2014 law has two officer-level grades, sous-lieutenant and aspirant, and the conscript category "élèves officiers du service national". This structure is consistent with the widely reported practice of placing university graduates on an officer-cadet track during service. Which diploma (licence, master or ingénieur) leads to which grade is set by internal regulation and could not be confirmed from an official source here.
- Because of Art. 46 and the 2018 DSN policy, an IT posting (transmissions, IT departments of regional commands, military schools) is plausible but discretionary. A CS graduate should declare his diploma and specialty accurately at the medical selection (Art. 21 requires it anyway) to improve the odds of a technical posting.

### Gaps
- No official or reliable press source was found giving: (a) the diploma-to-grade mapping (for example licence to aspirant, master or ingénieur to sous-lieutenant); (b) the length of basic or officer-cadet training for graduates (forum figures of a few months could not be verified); (c) the schools or centres where graduate conscripts train. The Art. 45 arrêté was not retrieved.
- No evidence was found either way on whether CS graduates are systematically placed in IT structures. There are no 2023-2026 reports of the 2018 DSN specialty-assignment plan being applied.
- The monthly allowance amounts per grade were not found.

## Q3. Converting national service into a military career (engagement / recrutement après service national)

### Takeaway
Art. 71 of Loi 14-06 gives anyone who has completed national service priority to compete for an ANP engagement contract, provided they meet the ordinary recruitment conditions. The main officer route for graduates in 2026 is the MDN's yearly "élèves officiers d'active sur titre / sur la base du diplôme universitaire" intake. Pre-registration is online only at preinscription.mdn.dz; the 2026 intake opened on 2 Aug 2026. The age ceiling is 19 plus the number of study years the diploma requires, and candidates must be single. That ceiling (about 24 for a master, 22 for a licence) will often have passed by the time a graduate finishes national service.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 71: "Le citoyen ayant satisfait aux obligations du service national bénéficie de la priorité à concourir à un contrat d'engagement au sein de l'Armée Nationale Populaire sous réserve qu'il remplisse les conditions fixées par la législation et la réglementation en vigueur" ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)) [OFFICIAL].
- Art. 57: a "certificat de bonne conduite" is issued to anyone who completed service without disciplinary sanctions above 8 days of arrêts ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)). It is likely part of an engagement file (inference).
- Arts. 49, 59, 63: a conscript kept beyond 12 months (for medical reasons or "situations d'exception") is "assimilé, selon son grade, au militaire de carrière ou servant en vertu d'un contrat" and is paid the career or contract soldier's solde for his grade ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)). This is a retention mechanism, not a voluntary career route.
- Art. 58: after service, conscripts are placed in the reserve ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)). Under the 2022 reserve law, national-service veterans stay in the reserve for 25 years from release. The first 5 years are a "réserve disponible", followed by first and second reserve. Peacetime recalls for training are limited to 30 days per year ([TSA, 11 Aug 2022](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/reserve-militaire-les-categories-de-reservistes-concernees/)) [PRESS, reporting the JO].
- 2026 officer "sur titre" intake [PRESS reporting MDN]:
  - The MDN opened online pre-registration for officer cadets recruited on the basis of a university degree on 2 Aug 2026, via preinscription.mdn.dz only, with no paper filing at this stage. The age limit is 19 plus the years of study required for the specialty, and "le célibat demeure obligatoire" ([Algerie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/)).
  - Eligibility as summarised by Awras from the Guide 2026: Algerian nationality; single; at least 19 on 31 Dec 2026 plus the study years needed for the degree; Bac with an average of at least 12/20; the university diploma required for the specialty; passing the admission competition; physical, mental and psychological fitness. The initial file is the inquiry form downloaded after online registration, a copy of the diploma, a copy of the national ID card and a photo ([Awras, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.awras.com/?p=509063)).
  - The "Guide du recrutement 2026" (guide2026_Fr.pdf on mdn.dz) was published in early July 2026. It lists required diplomas, age criteria, documents, selection stages, and the training institutions for each force, including Gendarmerie Nationale and Garde Républicaine ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)).
  - Neither the Algerie360 nor the Awras article lists the specialties open to graduates, so whether informatique is among the 2026 "sur titre" specialties could not be confirmed ([Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/); [Awras](https://www.awras.com/?p=509063)).
- Other 2026 categories [PRESS]: sous-officiers contractuels require the 3rd year of secondary school (bacheliers have priority) and age 18-22. Hommes du rang (caporal or soldat) need a lower education level ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)).

### Inferences
- Age arithmetic for a CS graduate is the main constraint. The age at which the diploma was earned determines eligibility, not completion of national service.
  - Licence (3 years): about 22 at most on 31 Dec of the intake year.
  - Master or "ingénieur" (5 years): about 24 at most. This matches the Gendarmerie's 2024 rule of 24 at most for master holders (Q7).
  - A graduate who finishes a master at 23, then serves 12 months, is about 24-25 at release, at or past the limit. To keep the officer "sur titre" option open, the best sequence is to apply straight after graduating, while still under sursis or in the gap before call-up, rather than after national service. Under Art. 60, direct recruitment followed by 24 months of service also discharges the national service obligation.
- Art. 71 gives priority to compete, not a guaranteed post, and the ordinary age, celibacy and diploma rules still apply. For older graduates who have done national service, the realistic conversion routes are contract categories. Whether any 2026 category gives national-service veterans an age extension or a dedicated quota could not be confirmed.
- The celibacy requirement rules out married graduates for the officer "sur titre" route.

### Gaps
- The full Guide du recrutement 2026 (mdn.dz) could not be read: the server was unreachable. Unconfirmed items: the list of specialties (informatique?), whether women may apply to "sur titre", training school and length for "sur titre" officers, grade on graduation, length of the initial engagement contract, and any age bonus for those who completed national service.
- No official procedure for a conscript to sign an engagement during his 12 months of service was found, apart from Art. 71's priority after service.
- No 2023-2026 numbers were found on how many conscripts convert to careers.

## Q4. Programmes placing graduates (CS) in technical/IT structures, research centres or military schools as instructors during national service

### Takeaway
No source found documents a formal programme that places graduate conscripts in IT structures, research centres, or as instructors at military schools. The only relevant items are Art. 46 of the law (adapted training by posting) and a 2018 DSN plan to assign conscripts by specialty.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 46 allows "formations adaptées selon leurs lieux d'affectation" ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)) [OFFICIAL].
- The 2018 DSN plan aimed to orient conscripts "suivant leurs spécialités", so that graduates "gain experience they can use after service". It was sent to the army chief of staff, and its implementation is not documented ([Echorouk FR, 1 Apr 2018](https://www.echoroukonline.com/service-national-appeles-orientes-suivant-leurs-specialites)) [PRESS, dated].
- The ANP trains its own IT officers through the bac route rather than through conscript graduates. The 2026 guide lists a Forces terrestres track: 1 year at Cherchell, then 3 years of university study at Cherchell ending in a Licence in Mécanique énergétique, Électronique or Informatique, then 1 application year. ENPEI (École Nationale Préparatoire aux Études d'Ingénieur) offers a 3-year track with specialisation in Informatique or Sciences de l'ingénieur ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)) [PRESS reporting MDN guide].

### Inferences
- Because the ANP grows its own informatique officers from bacheliers (Cherchell Licence Informatique, ENPEI), a civilian CS graduate's military IT role most likely comes either from a discretionary posting during national service or from the "sur titre" officer intake (Q3). A dedicated "conscript-instructor" scheme does not appear to be public.

### Gaps
- No public information was found on graduate conscripts serving at the École Militaire Polytechnique, the transmissions schools, MDN research centres, or as instructors. This may exist as internal practice but is undocumented in the sources checked. Forum claims were not found in this pass.

## Q5. Practical steps: recensement, service national offices, convocation, documents, carte, online services

### Takeaway
The sequence is: register in the year of turning 17 at the APC or consulate, receive the attestation de recensement, attend the medical selection when convoked (filing any sursis or dispense request there), receive the call-up order at 19 or at the end of the sursis, incorporate, and receive the carte du service national (plus a certificat de bonne conduite) on release. Citizens deal with the "centres / bureaux du service national" under the MDN. No official online service for checking national-service status was found. The only verified MDN online portal is preinscription.mdn.dz, which handles recruitment, not service national status.

### Cited Findings
- Recensement at the commune of residence or a consulate, for those turning 17 that year, between 2 Jan and 30 Sep, with an attestation issued (Arts. 10-13). A legal guardian can register an absent minor (Art. 11) ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)) [OFFICIAL].
- Medical selection is compulsory. The sursis, dispense or report request is filed there, and a dispense filing gets a receipt (Arts. 16, 19, 22). A citizen who develops a new illness can ask for a confirmatory medical exam before incorporation (Art. 23) ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).
- Call-up order and travel order (ordre d'appel, ordre de route) are delivered directly or through the Gendarmerie (Arts. 32-34). Incorporation = joining the unit, passing the incorporation medical exam and receiving an "avis d'incorporation" (Art. 41) ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).
- On release: a "carte du service national" (Art. 56) and a "certificat de bonne conduite" if no sanction exceeded 8 days of arrêts (Art. 57). There is a flat-rate travel refund for the selection, incorporation and release trips (Art. 65), and free meals at the medical selection (Art. 66) ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)).
- Documents (consular practice, Canberra embassy) [OFFICIAL]:
  - Recensement: birth certificate in Arabic, copy of the consular registration card, 3 photos, completed form.
  - Student sursis, first request: form, 4 photos, current birth certificate, proof of enrolment.
  - Yearly renewal: form, the existing "carte de sursis", and proof of continued enrolment. Apply at the start of each academic year.
  - No online application system; PDF forms are filed in person or by mail.
  ([Algerian Embassy Canberra](https://embcanberra.mfa.gov.dz/consular-services/national-military-service))
- In Algeria, the June 2024 exemption notice sent eligible men to the "centres de service national" with an ID copy and 2 photos ([Algerie360, 19 Jun 2024](https://www.algerie360.com/service-national-tebboune-donne-la-carte-militaire-aux-jeunes-nes-avant-cette-date/)) [PRESS].
- Online: the MDN recruitment pre-registration portal is preinscription.mdn.dz ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/); [Algerie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/)). The MDN has service-national pages such as [recensement29_fr.php](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/service/recensement29_fr.php), [medical_fr.php](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/service/medical_fr.php) and [dcssm2026fr.php](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/service/dcssm2026fr.php). These appeared in search results but returned 503 here, so their content is unverified.
- The government's unified e-services portal "Dzair Digital Services" (dzds.dz) offers civil-status and other documents. Press coverage does not list any service-national procedure in its catalogue ([Algerie360 on dzds.dz](https://www.algerie360.com/acte-de-naissance-fiche-familiale-la-plateforme-dzds-dz-simplifie-le-quotidien-des-algeriens/); [Algerie360, Dzair Digital Services](https://www.algerie360.com/dzair-digital-services-documents-en-ligne/)) [PRESS].

### Inferences
- A CS student has to renew the sursis every academic year, usually at the service national office covering his residence. Missing the medical selection makes him "apte d'office" and he loses the right to request a dispense (Art. 19). Being 25 without recensement or medical selection makes him insoumis (Art. 35).

### Gaps
- No official online service (MDN or dzds.dz) for checking national-service status or downloading a status certificate could be verified. Forum claims of such a service were not checked.
- The exact current documents for a sursis inside Algeria (as opposed to consulates), and whether the "carte de sursis" is still issued on paper, were not confirmed from MDN pages.
- The exact current name of the local office ("Bureau du Service National" vs "Centre du Service National") could not be confirmed. Press uses "centres de service national".

## Q6. Women: does national service apply only to men? How can women CS graduates enter?

### Takeaway
The text of Loi 14-06 is gender-neutral ("tous les citoyens algériens âgés de 19 ans"), but in practice only men are recensés and called. An MDN officer has publicly called for a political decision to include young women, which confirms they are not currently called. Women CS graduates enter through voluntary recruitment instead: the Gendarmerie officer route for master holders was open to women in 2024, and the Garde Républicaine sets height minimums for women.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 3 of Loi 14-06 says "tous les citoyens algériens âgés de dix-neuf (19) ans révolus", with no gender restriction in the text ([JO n°48/2014](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2014/dz-government-gazette-dated-2014-08-10-no-48.pdf)) [OFFICIAL].
- Ennahar (date not stated) quotes Lt-Col Debiche Tarek of the MDN: Loi 14-06 does not distinguish men and women, and he called on APN deputies to take a political decision to include girls in national service. The article describes national service as a "school for men" ([Ennahar](https://www.ennaharonline.com/لا-معريفة-في-الإعفاء-ولارمي-للفتيات-ب/)) [PRESS, undated].
- Carnegie (2018) found no post-independence policy to conscript women; women are mainly used in information and communication roles, as soldiers or civilians ([Carnegie Diwan, Mar 2018](https://carnegieendowment.org/ar/middle-east/diwan/2018/03/girls-interrupted)) [ANALYSIS, dated].
- Women in voluntary routes:
  - Gendarmerie officers for master holders (2024): "males and females", height at least 1.70 m (M) and 1.60 m (F), at most 24 on 31/12/2024 ([sfnemploi, 18 Jun 2024](https://sfnemploi.com/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B5%D9%81%D9%88%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B1%D9%83-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-2024-%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7/)) [JOB SITE].
  - Garde Républicaine officer route (bac): 1.78 m for men and 1.65 m for women ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)) [PRESS].
- The ANP chief of staff holds yearly ceremonies for International Women's Day honouring ANP servicewomen ([Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/41864); [El Bilad](https://www.elbilad.net/national/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%B4%D9%86%D9%82%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D9%8A%D9%87%D9%86%D8%A6-%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B4-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B9%D8%A8%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A3%D8%A9-138073)) [OFFICIAL MEDIA].

### Inferences
- A woman CS graduate has no national-service route at all. Her entry points are the yearly MDN "sur titre" officer intake (if open to women for the relevant specialty), the Gendarmerie's graduate officer recruitment (open to women in 2024), or civilian employment at MDN (not researched here). Women are not subject to Art. 7's status requirement in practice, which lets them start civilian or military careers earlier than male peers.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 official statement was found confirming in writing that women are excluded from recensement. The exclusion is practice-based, inferred from MDN officials' statements and the lack of female call-ups.
- Whether the 2026 "sur titre" officer intake accepts women for all specialties, including informatique, is unconfirmed because the guide was not readable.

## Q7. Gendarmerie Nationale and Garde Républicaine: graduate (informatique) recruitment, cyber units (CPLCIC), conditions, ranks, process

### Takeaway
The Gendarmerie Nationale is under the MDN and recruits through the MDN system, with its own section of the MDN website at mdn.dz/site_cgn/. Graduate entry as an officer has been open to holders of a master or ingénieur d'État, and in some years a licence académique, in specialties the Gendarmerie needs. Informatique, electrical engineering and electronics have been explicitly named. The 2024 conditions were: at most 24 years old, men or women, 1.70 m (M) or 1.60 m (F), two years of training. The Gendarmerie's cybercrime centre (CPLCIC) exists and is growing (2,838 cases in 2021, 4,600 in 2022), but no dedicated public recruitment channel into it was found. Staff appear to come from general Gendarmerie recruitment, followed by specialised training at home and abroad. No CS-specific graduate route into the Garde Républicaine was found.

### Cited Findings
**Status and website**
- The Gendarmerie nationale is placed under the authority of the Minister of National Defence. Its national institute of criminalistics and criminology (INCC-GN) is in Bouchaoui, west of Algiers (search summary of [Force Publique / FIEP 2023 profile](https://www.force-publique.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/2023-Algerie-fr.pdf)) [INSTITUTIONAL, not fetched directly].
- The Gendarmerie's official web presence is the MDN subsite "site_cgn". For example, a 24 May 2026 news item on a Gendarmerie colloquium on fighting cybercrime ([mdn.dz/site_cgn, 24 May 2026](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cgn/sommaire/actualites/fr/actualite24052026.php)) [OFFICIAL, seen in search results only; site unreachable]. The domain "mgn.dz" was not verified.

**Graduate officer recruitment (informatique)**
- 2024: master holders can join as Gendarmerie officers, men and women. Conditions: at most 24 on 31/12/2024; height at least 1.70 m (M) or 1.60 m (F); training lasts 2 years ([sfnemploi, 18 Jun 2024](https://sfnemploi.com/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B5%D9%81%D9%88%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B1%D9%83-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-2024-%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7/)) [JOB SITE, citing an MDN/university document].
- An MDN document reproduced by a job site (year unclear) states the officer category needs at least an ingénieur d'État, master or licence académique in specialties meeting Gendarmerie needs. It includes one year of specialised training at the Gendarmerie's judicial police school in Sétif, and the file requires copies of the diploma ([jobs4dz](https://www.jobs4dz.com/%d9%85%d9%84%d9%81-%d9%88%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%a7%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%b5%d9%81%d9%88%d9%81-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d8%b1%d9%83-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b7%d9%86%d9%8a-20/)) [AGGREGATOR]. This conflicts with the 2026 guide's naming of the École de Police Judiciaire at Isser for the bac track (below). The Sétif mention may be older or may refer to a different school.
- Ennahar (date not shown) reported a recruitment competition open to holders of an ingénieur d'État or master in informatique, alongside electrical engineering and electronics ([Ennahar](https://www.ennaharonline.com/مسابقـــة-لتوظيف-طلبــــــــــة-آ/)) [PRESS, undated].
- 2019-2020: the Gendarmerie command opened more than 8,000 posts including university graduates as officers, sous-officiers and gendarmes ([Echorouk](https://www.echoroukonline.com/8-آلاف-منصب-عمل-في-قوات-الدرك)) [PRESS, dated 2019]. The first-ever competition for LMD licence holders as contract sous-officiers was also reported ([Ennahar](https://www.ennaharonline.com/مسابقـــة-لتوظيف-طلبــــــــــة-آ/)) [PRESS, undated].
- 2026 officer route for bacheliers: Bac average of at least 12/20 from any stream. One year at Cherchell, three years at the École Supérieure de la Gendarmerie Nationale (Zéralda) for a Licence en Droit et Sécurité Publique, then one specialisation year at the École de Police Judiciaire (Isser) ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)) [PRESS reporting MDN guide].
- 2026 graduate officers ("sur titre") apply through the common MDN intake at preinscription.mdn.dz, opened 2 Aug 2026. The guide covers training institutions of the Gendarmerie and Garde Républicaine. Conditions: age 19 plus study years, single, Bac of at least 12/20 ([Algerie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/); [Awras, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.awras.com/?p=509063)). Whether Gendarmerie "sur titre" posts in 2026 included informatique is unconfirmed.

**Non-officer Gendarmerie recruitment (2025-2026)**
- 2025: the Gendarmerie recruitment drive ran exclusively through the MDN digital platform (search summary of [Algerie360, 2025](https://www.algerie360.com/la-gendarmerie-nationale-recrute-2025-conditions-dates-et-procedure-a-suivre/)) [PRESS].
- Feb 2026: sous-officiers and contract hommes du rang. Conditions: Algerian, single, at least 1.70 m, aged 18-22 on 31 Dec. A temporary one-year measure accepts completion of the 4th year of middle school, with priority to vocational-diploma holders. Applications go to the nearest Gendarmerie unit, as the regional groupements began receiving 2026-2027 files. Required: birth extract no. 12, school certificate, photo, ID copy and residence certificate. Training is 6 months at the instruction centres of Aïn M'lila, Touggourt, Jijel and Djenien Bourezg ([Algerie360, 27 Feb 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/la-gendarmerie-nationale-recrute-2026-une-chance-inedite-pour-cette-categorie-de-candidats/)) [PRESS]. This route is not aimed at graduates, and its age cap of 22 excludes most CS master holders.

**Cybercrime: CPLCIC**
- The "Centre de prévention et de lutte contre la criminalité informatique et la cybercriminalité" (CPLCIC) belongs to the Gendarmerie nationale. It handled 909 cybercrime cases in 2017, including privacy violations, blackmail, hacking of data and systems, identity theft and fraud. It follows a prevention, vigilance and digital-security strategy ([TSA, 17 Jan 2018](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/cybercriminalite-909-affaires-enregistrees-en-2017/)) [PRESS, dated]. The article gives no creation date, location or staffing.
- Colonel Djamel Berdjem is named as head of a Gendarmerie "Centre de prévention contre la cybercriminalité", reporting about 2,500 cybercrimes over three years ([Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/le-colonel-djamel-berdjem-pres-de-2-500-cybercrimes-enregistres-en-trois-ans/)) [PRESS, date not established]. This is probably the same body under a shorter name.
- Gendarmerie cybercrime cases rose from 2,838 in 2021 to 4,600 in 2022 (search summary of [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/cybercriminalite-en-algerie-les-mises-en-garde-de-la-gendarmerie/)) [PRESS].
- The Gendarmerie's criminal department announced a special cybercrime service with officers trained in Algeria and abroad (search summary of [Echorouk EN](https://www.echoroukonline.com/cyber-criminality-undermines-algeriaaes-security-experts-say)) [PRESS, undated].
- In 2024 the French Gendarmerie offered to share cybercrime expertise with the Algerian Gendarmerie during a meeting in Algiers ([Gendarmerie nationale (France), 2024](https://www.gendarmerie.interieur.gouv.fr/gendinfo/actualites/2024/cooperation-internationale-rencontre-a-alger-entre-les-forces-de-gendarmerie-francaise-et-algerienne)) [OFFICIAL, foreign].
- The Gendarmerie held a colloquium on fighting cybercrime on 24 May 2026 ([mdn.dz/site_cgn](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cgn/sommaire/actualites/fr/actualite24052026.php)) [OFFICIAL, from search listing].

**Garde Républicaine**
- 2026 officer route (bac): average of at least 12/20, height 1.78 m (men) or 1.65 m (women), 5-year officer programme ([Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)) [PRESS].

**Algerian vs French sources:** many search results on "Gendarmerie nationale recrutement officier universitaire / informatique" are from the French Gendarmerie (gendarmerie.interieur.gouv.fr: OGU, OSC-S, COFRA and similar). They do not apply to Algeria and were excluded.

### Inferences
- For a male CS master or ingénieur graduate, the most direct route to cyber or IT work in the Gendarmerie is the graduate officer intake: MDN "sur titre" via preinscription.mdn.dz, age 24 at most for a 5-year diploma, single. This is followed by about 2 years of training (per 2024 conditions), then possible posting to the CPLCIC, regional cyber units or the INCC-GN. Since direct ANP or Gendarmerie service of 24 months or more discharges national service (Art. 60), applying straight after graduation avoids doing national service separately.
- Graduates already past 24 have weaker options. Art. 71 gives national-service veterans priority for contract engagement, but no published Gendarmerie category for older IT graduates was found.
- The Garde Républicaine's strict height minimums (1.78 m for men) make it a narrower option than the Gendarmerie for most candidates.

### Gaps
- No official 2025 or 2026 Gendarmerie notice listing informatique among the specialties for graduate officers was retrieved. The informatique evidence comes from an undated Ennahar article and an aggregator.
- The creation decree, headquarters, structure and staffing model of the CPLCIC were not found, nor was any CPLCIC-specific recruitment notice.
- The grade at the end of training for Gendarmerie graduate officers (presumably sous-lieutenant), the minimum contract or service length, and pay were not found.
- No Garde Républicaine graduate (sur titre) informatique route was found.
- The Gendarmerie school for graduate officers' specialised year is unresolved: one aggregator says Sétif, while the 2026 guide names Isser for bacheliers.
