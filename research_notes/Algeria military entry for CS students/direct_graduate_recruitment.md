# Direct recruitment of university graduates (especially informatique) into the Algerian military (ANP / MDN), without a multi-year military school

Research date: 2026-11-25. Scope: Algeria only.

**Access caveat for the report writer:** every page on mdn.dz and preinscription.mdn.dz returned HTTP 503 or timed out from this research environment, and several Algerian press sites (akhbardzair.dz, maghrebinfo.dz, lapatrienews.dz, elaane.dz) only showed a "DZSecurity" bot-check page. Official MDN text below therefore comes from (a) search-engine summaries of mdn.dz pages, labelled "via search snippet", and (b) Algerian press reports quoting the MDN (Algerie360, Echorouk, L'Express). The full MDN Guide de recrutement 2026 PDF could not be read. Treat any detail not explicitly attributed to MDN as unofficial.

## 1. Graduates as career officers ("officiers de carrière / d'active recrutés sur titre", "الطلبة الضباط العاملين على أساس الشهادة الجامعية"): diplomas, rank, training, engagement

### Takeaway
The main direct channel for Licence, Master and Ingénieur d'État holders is the annual recruitment of "élèves officiers d'active / de carrière sur titre" (officer cadets recruited on their degree). The age limit is 19 plus the minimum years needed for the degree, and candidates must be single. For 2026, online pre-registration opened on 2 August 2026. Training is short compared with the 5-year bac route: the Gendarmerie version is officially 2 years, starting with 1 year at the Académie Militaire de Cherchell. The rank given for each diploma (Licence to sous-lieutenant, Master/Ingénieur to lieutenant) appears only in unofficial sources and is not confirmed by MDN text.

### Cited Findings
**Campaign and portal (2026)**
- On Sunday 2 August 2026 the MDN launched electronic pre-registration for "élèves officiers de carrière sur titre". It targets "les candidats déjà titulaires d'un diplôme universitaire". Pre-registration is only through the official portal preinscription.mdn.dz, "sans possibilité de dépôt physique de dossier à ce stade". The article says "Chaque filière impose ses propres critères" and sends readers to the Guide du recrutement 2026 on www.mdn.dz for specialties and documents. — [Algerie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/)
- The MDN's 2026 recruitment guide for ANP training establishments was published around 3 July 2026 and is linked as "guide2026_Fr.pdf". For officers recruited sur titre, "l'âge limite est fixé à 19 ans, majoré du nombre d'années d'études requises", and "Le célibat demeure obligatoire pour ces catégories." — [Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)
- Echorouk's summary of the 2026 guide (3 July 2026) gives the university-degree track as: age 19 plus the minimum years of study needed for the degree, counted at 31 December 2026; single; pass the admission competition; physical fitness. It also says pre-registration is "حصريا عبر الموقع الإلكتروني لوزارة الدفاع" (only through the MDN website). — [Echorouk, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%83%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A8)
- A search-engine result for the graduate-officer pre-registration shows a portal sub-path, preinscription.mdn.dz/fs/. It could not be opened to confirm. — [preinscription.mdn.dz/fs](https://preinscription.mdn.dz/fs/) (via search snippet)

**Official MDN conditions (via search snippets; pages unreachable)**
- MDN "Conditions de recrutement" pages for the Naval and Land Forces state the sur-titre rule: "être âgé de 19 ans, majorés du nombre d'années minimum requis pour l'obtention du diplôme universitaire (au 31 décembre 2025)", be single, and pass the admission competition. One ANP page adds that "la liste des spécialités demandées sera publiée ultérieurement" (the list of specialties is published later, per campaign). — [MDN Forces Navales, Conditions de recrutement](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cfn/sommaire/formation/off_fr.php); [MDN Forces Terrestres, Condition de recrutement des officiers](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cft/sommaire/recrutement%20et%20formation/amc_5_fr.php) (via search snippet)
- **Gendarmerie Nationale officers sur titre (official MDN page):** minimum diploma is an "ingéniorat d'État, master ou licence académique" in the required specialties. Maximum age at 31 December of the current year is **24 for ingénieur d'État / master holders and 22 for licence holders**. Training lasts **2 years**: year 1 is common basic military training at the Académie Militaire de Cherchell, and year 2 is specialised (professional) training. One search summary names the École de Police Judiciaire at Yesser for year 2. — [MDN Gendarmerie, Officiers d'active (FR)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cgn/sommaire/form_rec/recrutement/officiers/off_fr.php); [EN version](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cgn/sommaire/form_rec/recrutement/officiers/off_en.php); [AR version](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cgn/sommaire/form_rec/recrutement/officiers/off_ar.php) (via search snippet)
- MDN pages also exist for air-force officer recruitment and for air-defence (DAT) officer recruitment. Their content was not retrievable. — [MDN Forces Aériennes, officier](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cfa/sommaire/formation/recrutement/officier/officier_fr.php); [MDN Forces de Défense Aérienne du Territoire, officier](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cfdat/sommaire/recrutement/officier_fr.php)

**Rank given per diploma (unofficial only)**
- defense-arab.com (26 Aug 2023) says Master or Ingénieur holders in accepted specialties can be recruited directly at the rank of **ملازم أول** (lieutenant), age at most 24. Licence holders join at **ملازم** (sous-lieutenant), age at most 22. Doctorate holders are not mentioned. It cites no official text. — [Defense-Arab, 26 Aug 2023](https://defense-arab.com/news/76330/)
- JOBSdz, an aggregator, says specialised training for graduates lasts 1 to 2 years and that they graduate as ملازم. It gives a graduate-officer age range of 19–23 (or "23 to 25" in another post), which conflicts with the official formula. — [JOBSdz, Oct 2026](https://jobsdz.com/fr/2026/10/nouvelles-annonces-de-recrutement-au-ministere-de-la-defense-nationale-algerien/) (via search summary; aggregator)

**Historical informatique-specific example (2013)**
- In August 2013 the MDN's Direction des Industries Militaires recruited holders of a **diplôme d'ingénieur d'État** in electrical engineering, chemical engineering and **computer engineering ("هندسة الإعلام الآلي")** as **"طلبة ضباط عاملين"** (career officer cadets). Conditions were: age at most 24 at 31 Dec 2013, IT skills, physical fitness, civil rights, and ranking by grade average. The deadline was 15 August. The paper dossier was: written application; birth certificates (form 12) of the candidate and the father; civil-status card; nationality certificate; criminal record (bulletin no. 3); certified diploma copy; residence certificate; **certificate of single status**; 16 photos; general and chest medical certificates. — [dzairjob blog citing Echorouk, Aug 2013](https://dzairjob.blogspot.com/2013/08/blog-post.html) (old; secondary)

**Contrast: the bac route that the user wants to avoid**
- The standard bac route at Cherchell (Forces Terrestres) is a 5-year cursus: 1 common military year, then 3 university years ending in a Licence in mechanical energy, electronics or **computer science**, then 1 application year. It is open to 2026 bac holders (Sciences Expérimentales, Maths, Technique-Maths) with at least "Assez Bien", aged 18–21. — [Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/); [Echorouk, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%83%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A8)
- The MDN's École Militaire Polytechnique page says its master's complement is open only to career officers who already hold an engineering diploma. — [MDN État-Major ANP, formation](https://www.mdn.dz/site_em_anp/sommaire/formation/fr/emp-fr.php) (via search snippet)

### Inferences
- Applying the official "19 + minimum years of study" rule gives about 22 for a Licence (3 years, LMD), 24 for a Master or Ingénieur d'État (5 years), and around 27 for a Doctorat (8 years in LMD). This matches the Gendarmerie's explicit 22/24 limits. A CS student who is older than this, or married, is excluded from this channel.
- The likely shape of the channel: a short military course of about 1 year of basic training at Cherchell (or at the force's own school), plus specialisation, as in the Gendarmerie's 2 years. After that the cadet is commissioned as a career officer. This is much shorter than the 5-year bac route. It is still a school phase, so "without any military school" is not literally possible as an officer.
- Specialties vary by year. Informatique has appeared before (2013 DIM computer engineering), but the 2026 specialty list was not visible.
- The commitment (minimum years of service, or reimbursement if leaving) for sur-titre officers was not found. Career officers are, by definition, under the statut général (Ordonnance 06-02), not a fixed-term contract.

### Gaps
- No official MDN text was retrievable confirming the rank by diploma (sous-lieutenant vs lieutenant), the treatment of Doctorat holders, the exact training length per force, or the minimum service commitment.
- The 2026 list of specialties (whether informatique, réseaux, cybersécurité, IA or data is included) could not be read, because the guide PDF on mdn.dz was unreachable.
- The closing date of the Aug 2026 sur-titre campaign was not found.

## 2. Graduates as contract officers ("ضباط متعاقدون / officiers contractuels")

### Takeaway
No 2022–2026 source was found describing a campaign recruiting university graduates as contract officers (officiers contractuels / ضباط متعاقدون) in the ANP. Recent MDN contract channels found are for **sous-officiers contractuels** and **militaires du rang contractuels** only.

### Cited Findings
- Searches in French and Arabic for "officiers contractuels" and "ضباط متعاقدين" returned only the sur-titre career-officer campaign, sous-officiers contractuels and hommes du rang contractuels. — [Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/); [Echorouk, 23 Nov 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D9%81%D8%AA%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA)
- The legal framework is Ordonnance 06-02 of 28 Feb 2006 (statut général des personnels militaires). It was amended in Dec 2019 (art. 30 bis, a 5-year ban on political activity after leaving service) and by Ordonnance 21-06 in 2021. The contract-length clauses were not visible in search results. — [Elbilad](https://www.elbilad.net/national/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B3%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84-%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0-39489); [Radio Algérie, 10 Nov 2019](https://radioalgerie.dz/news/ar/article/20191110/184187.html) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- Officer status for a graduate appears to come mainly through the career (sur titre) route. A contract-officer status for graduates may exist in law, but no evidence was found that it is used as a public recruitment channel today.

### Gaps
- No source found for the existence, contract length, rank or conditions of an "officier contractuel" track for graduates. The text of Ordonnance 06-02 (JORADP) on contract categories could not be retrieved, and joradp.dz was not tested because mdn.dz-class sites were blocked.

## 3. Sous-officiers contractuels and militaires du rang contractuels (BTS / technicien supérieur / Licence informatique)

### Takeaway
The contract NCO channel targets the 3AS / bac level, with ages 18–22. The contract rank-and-file channel targets lower levels. Both are open to a BTS or Licence holder only if they fall within the age limit; no channel was found that gives a higher NCO rank for a BTS or Licence in informatique. The latest rank-and-file campaign opened on 23 November 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Sous-officiers contractuels (2026 guide):** "le niveau de la 3ème année secondaire complète est requis avec une priorité aux bacheliers", age 18–22. Training is 1 year for the land forces and navy and 2 years for the air force and Republican Guard. Special forces and armoured corps take 2 years; artillery and reconnaissance take 1 year. — [Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/); [Echorouk, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%83%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A8)
- MDN has dedicated pages for Air Force sous-officiers contractuels and for DAT sous-officiers. — [MDN Forces Aériennes, Sous-officiers contractuels](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cfa/sommaire/formation/recrutement/sofficier/sofficier_fr.php); [MDN DAT sous-officier (AR)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cfdat/sommaire/recrutement/sofficier_ar.php) (content not retrievable)
- **Militaires du rang contractuels (current campaign):** on Monday 23 Nov 2026 the MDN opened pre-registration for "رجال الصف المتعاقدين" for the land, air and naval forces, DAT, Gendarmerie, Republican Guard, and MDN central directorates/services, at **https://preinscription.mdn.dz/hdr/**. — [Echorouk, 23 Nov 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D9%81%D8%AA%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA)
- Aggregators reporting the same campaign give: Algerian nationality, age 18 to 22 at most at 31 Dec 2026, single, good physical, mental and psychological condition, and printing the pre-registration receipt for the dossier. — [Echorouk/aggregators via search summary](https://www.unlimited-news.com/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%AA%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%B1%D8%AC/) (unofficial)
- Rank-and-file entry levels (2026 guide): **caporal (عريف)** requires the BEM plus proof of 1st-year secondary level; **soldat (جندي)** requires at least 1st-year middle school. Vocational-training diploma holders get priority for both. Training is about 6 months in the navy and Republican Guard. — [Echorouk, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%83%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A8); [Algerie360, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)
- Unofficial (2023): **رقيب (sergent)** is open to anyone with a full 3AS, with priority to bac holders, aged 18–21. BTS/technicien supérieur is not mentioned. — [Defense-Arab, 26 Aug 2023](https://defense-arab.com/news/76330/)
- An aggregator says a 2025 DAT sous-officiers contractuels campaign had training at the École des cadres de la DAT "Chahid Ferrache Ahmed", Berrouaghia (Médéa). — [sfnemploi (via search snippet)](https://sfnemploi.com/%D9%81%D8%AA%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B5%D9%81%D9%88%D9%81-%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%88%D9%8A-%D8%B9/) (unofficial)

### Inferences
- For a BTS/TS informatique holder or a young Licence holder (22 or under), the contract-NCO channel is open in principle, but the diploma brings no rank advantage in any source found. A Licence holder under 22 would generally be better served by the sur-titre officer channel.
- Contract length for sous-officiers and hommes du rang contractuels was not found.

### Gaps
- No source on contract duration or renewal terms for sous-officiers or hommes du rang contractuels.
- No source confirming whether a BTS/TS in informatique gives priority, a specific specialty (e.g., transmissions/informatique) or a higher entry rank.
- The deadline of the Nov 2026 rank-and-file campaign was not found.

## 4. Personnels Civils Assimilés (PCA, "المستخدمون المدنيون الشبيهون"): civilian engineers and informaticiens at the MDN

### Takeaway
PCA recruitment is the most direct route for informatique graduates who want MDN employment without military training. It is a civilian competitive recruitment (concours) by wilaya, run through preinscription.mdn.dz/pca/. Campaigns have explicitly included Ingénieur d'État/Master en informatique (systèmes d'information, maintenance), DEUA informatique/réseaux, Technicien supérieur informatique/réseaux and technicien informatique. Requirements are nationality, a regularised national-service situation, residence in the post's wilaya and passing the concours. Age caps were 35 or 40 by category in 2022. There is no single-status requirement in the sources.

### Cited Findings
- **Feb 2025 campaign:** on Thursday 6 Feb 2025 the MDN opened PCA pre-registration. Conditions: Algerian nationality; "Avoir régularisé sa situation vis-à-vis du service national"; diploma in the required specialty; residence in the wilaya where the post is open; "Réussir le concours d'admission". Documents (scanned and uploaded): pre-registration form, biometric ID card, diploma copy, national-service status certificate, residence certificate for the wilaya, and a recent photo. "Aucun dossier papier ne sera accepté." Portal: https://preinscription.mdn.dz (and preinscription.mdn.dz/pca/). No closing date was given. — [Algerie360, 6 Feb 2025](https://www.algerie360.com/le-mdn-recrute-des-personnels-civils-assimiles-modalites-et-documents-requis/)
- **2025 IT specialties listed:**
  - "الدراسات الجامعية التطبيقية في الإعلام الآلي" (DEUA informatique), with a networks option and an informatique de gestion option.
  - "مهندس دولة أو ماستر في الإعلام الآلي" (Ingénieur d'État or Master in informatique), with options in "أنظمة المعلومات" (information systems) and "صيانة" (maintenance).
  - "تقني سامي في الإعلام الآلي + مستوى الثالثة ثانوي كاملة" (TS informatique plus a full 3AS), with a networks option.
  - "تقني في الإعلام الآلي" (technicien informatique), with a networks option.
  - No Licence in informatique was listed. — [a-onec, Feb 2025](https://www.a-onec.com/2025/02/mdn-dz-concours.html) (secondary; reproduces MDN announcement)
- **Nov 2022 campaign:** the MDN's Direction des personnels recruited PCA for the Commandements de forces, Directions and central services, and Commandements des Régions Militaires. Age limits at 31 Dec 2023 were **35 for catégories ≤ 6 and 40 for catégories ≥ 7**. Grades included ingénieurs d'État in many specialties, plus informatique posts for teachers (PEM), maîtres assistants, techniciens supérieurs and techniciens. Other conditions were the same as in 2025 (nationality, national service regularised, diploma, residence in the wilaya, concours). The form was downloadable at mdn.dz/.../recrutement/images/formulaire.pdf. Dossiers were due within 30 days of the announcement. — [L'Express (Algérie), 10 Nov 2022](https://www.lexpressquotidien.dz/2022/11/10/le-mdn-recrute-des-personnels-civils-assimiles/); also [Maghrebinfo, 8 Nov 2022](https://www.maghrebinfo.dz/2022/11/08/mdn-la-direction-des-personnels-recrute-des-personnels-civils-assimiles/) (not readable, bot wall)
- **Statute:** a presidential decree no. 23-143 (March 2023) sets the statute of civilian personnel of the MDN, distinguishing PCA from civilian contract staff. This appears in Journal Officiel no. 22 of 4 April 2023. — [JORADP no. 22, 4 Apr 2023 (gazettes.africa mirror)](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/dz/2023/dz-government-gazette-dated-2023-04-04-no-22.pdf) (via search summary; decree text not read directly. Verify number and title before citing.)
- An aggregator says (Jul and Oct 2026) that the MDN recruits civilians for administrative, technical, health and research posts by concours, including informatique engineers. It gives no 2026 dates. — [JOBSdz, Oct 2026](https://jobsdz.com/fr/2026/10/nouvelles-offres-demploi-au-ministere-de-la-defense-nationale/) (aggregator)

### Inferences
- PCA is a civilian career status within the MDN. Candidates are hired by grade and category (catégorie/صنف) on the public-service grid, and an ingénieur d'État would sit in a higher category (≥ 7, so the 40-year age cap in 2022). There is no military training or rank. The exact category and index for "ingénieur d'État en informatique" under decree 23-143 was not confirmed.
- PCA requires a settled national-service status. In practice this means having completed, been exempted from, or been deferred from the Service National, so it suits graduates who are older or past the military age windows.
- Frequency: campaigns observed in Nov 2022 and Feb 2025. No confirmed 2026 PCA campaign was found.

### Gaps
- No source found for PCA pay grade, salary, or the exact category of each informatique grade.
- No source on the stages after pre-registration (written test content, interview, security inquiry) beyond "concours d'admission".
- The 2025 age limits were not found (only 2022's 35/40).
- No confirmed 2026 PCA campaign.

## 5. Common conditions: nationality, age, marital status, medical, record, national service, gender

### Takeaway
Across military channels the constants are Algerian nationality, single status (officers sur titre, sous-officiers and hommes du rang), strict age windows (officers 19 plus years of study; NCOs 18–22), physical fitness and passing a competition with psychotechnical and sports tests. PCA drops the single-status rule but requires a regularised national-service situation and wilaya residence. Women are eligible for at least some officer tracks.

### Cited Findings
- Officers sur titre: single, age 19 plus required years of study, pass the admission competition, physical fitness. — [Echorouk, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%83%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A8); [Algerie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/)

## 6. Application process: portals, recruitment offices, dossier, selection steps, timeline

### Takeaway
All recent MDN campaigns are online-first through **preinscription.mdn.dz**, with sub-portals such as /fs/ (graduate officers, unconfirmed), /hdr/ (hommes du rang) and /pca/ (civilians). Paper dossiers are not accepted at pre-registration. The yearly cycle visible in 2025–2026 is: the guide is published in early July, officer sur-titre pre-registration opens in early August, hommes du rang open in late November, and PCA runs on an irregular schedule (Nov 2022, Feb 2025).

### Cited Findings
