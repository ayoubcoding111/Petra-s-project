# Admission routes into Algerian military schools (ANP / MDN) for computer science students and graduates

Research date: 2026-11-25. Method note: the official MDN site (mdn.dz, including the 2026 recruitment guide PDF and preinscription.mdn.dz) returned HTTP 503 or timed out from the research environment. Content attributed to mdn.dz below comes from search-engine extracts of those official pages (quoted text where available), cross-checked against Algerian press (Algerie360, Echorouk, Radio Algérie, El Moudjahid, L'Expression, APS). Anything from forums, job-aggregator or commercial "orientation" sites is labeled UNOFFICIAL.

## Q1. Which ANP schools are relevant to CS, and what do they offer (specialties, degree, length, rank)?

### Takeaway
The main CS-relevant ANP institutions are the Académie Militaire de Cherchell (AMC), which runs Licence/engineer tracks in informatique for land-forces officers; the École Supérieure des Transmissions (EST) Koléa (signals, electronic warfare, information systems, with Licence and Master degrees); the École Militaire Polytechnique (EMP) Bordj El Bahri (officer-engineers including informatique, a "master complémentaire" and doctoral training); and ENPEI Rouiba, the 3-year preparatory school with an "informatique" track that feeds EMP. Naval (télécommunications) and air-defence (radiotechnique) schools are adjacent options. No ANP school dedicated to "cyber" was found. A civilian École Nationale Supérieure de Cybersécurité was created in 2024, and the sources disagree on whether it is under MESRS alone or jointly with MDN.

### Cited Findings
**Académie Militaire de Cherchell (AMC), land forces (Forces Terrestres)**
- The official 2026 guide page states land-forces officer training includes "Formation d'ingénieur en «Mécanique énergétique» ou «Informatique» à l'Académie Militaire de Cherchell". — [MDN, Guide de recrutement 2026 (search extract)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/recrutement/recrutement_fr.php)
- Official land-forces page (search extract): officer training lasts five years, including "Trois (03) années de formation universitaire sanctionnées par un diplôme de Licence académique en (mécanique énergétique, électronique, informatique)". — [MDN Forces Terrestres recruitment page](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cft/sommaire/recrutement%20et%20formation/recrutement_fr.php)
- Press summary of the 2026 guide: land-forces officers take 5 years (1 common fundamental year at Cherchell, 3 university years for a Licence académique in Mécanique énergétique, Électronique or Informatique, then 1 application year). AMC also hosts the common first year for several other branches (navy, gendarmerie, logistics). — [Algerie360, 3 July 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)
- Conflict: the official page extract says "Formation d'ingénieur" in informatique, while the other official extract and Algerie360 describe a "Licence académique" plus an application year. Both phrasings appear on MDN pages, so the exact degree title (Licence vs Ingénieur) is unresolved. — [MDN guide 2026](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/recrutement/recrutement_fr.php); [MDN Forces Terrestres](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cft/sommaire/recrutement%20et%20formation/recrutement_fr.php)

**École Supérieure des Transmissions "Abdelhafidh Boussouf", Koléa (Tipaza)**
- Since October 2008 the former "École d'Application des Transmissions" has been the École Supérieure des Transmissions, under the joint supervision of the MDN and the Ministry of Higher Education (MESRS). — [bac-story (UNOFFICIAL orientation site), via search extract](https://www.bac-story.com/university/speciality/signal-corps)
- Radio Algérie (open-day report, 22 July 2019): year 1 is common basic military training at AMC Cherchell. Years 2–4 are a 3-year LMD Licence in science and technology with three tracks: electronics, mathematics and computer science (إعلام آلي). A 2-year Master covers electronics, telecommunication systems and information-systems security ("أمن منظومات المعلومات", created 2018). After that comes 1 year of officer specialization in signals (إشارة), electronic warfare (حرب إلكترونية) or "information and command systems" (منظومات الإعلام والقيادة). Graduates are commissioned as lieutenant (ملازم). A doctorate was planned "in the future". The first Master class graduated in 2015. (2019 information.) — [Radio Algérie, 22 July 2019](https://radioalgerie.dz/news/ar/article/20190722/175241.html)
- June 2025 graduation (MDN, 23 June 2025): the promotions included the 9th Master "Électronique et Systèmes d'information", the 6th Master "Systèmes d'Information" and the 3rd Master "techniques avancées de traitement du signal". The long cycle is described as 4 years, including 1 year of basic military training at AMC. — [MDN news, 23 June 2025 (search extract)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/actualites/fr/2025/juin/est23062025fr.php)
- Older graduation coverage mentions the domains of transmissions, electronic warfare, "systèmes informatique et commandement" and "sécurité des systèmes de renseignements", and Masters in "électronique et systèmes de communications" and "systèmes de renseignements" (2022–2023 and earlier). — [APS, EST Koléa graduation](https://www.aps.dz/algerie/157505-anp-sortie-de-nouvelles-promotions-de-l-ecole-superieure-des-transmissions-de-kolea); [El Moudjahid, "Prête pour la guerre électronique"](https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/ecole-superieure-des-transmissions-de-kolea-prete-pour-la-guerre-electronique-219961)
- Echorouk (15 Aug 2026), reporting on the 2026 intake, names a "Central School of Signals and Information Systems" among the schools on offer. The French or Arabic official name is not given in the English version, and it is unclear whether this is EST Koléa or a separate or renamed school. — [Echorouk, 15 Aug 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/military-specializations-attract-high-achieving-baccalaureate-graduates)

**École Militaire Polytechnique (EMP), Bordj El Bahri**
- Founded in 1967. Admission to the regular engineering cycle is mostly after the preparatory classes of ENPEI. — [Wikipedia FR, École militaire polytechnique](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89cole_militaire_polytechnique)
- The June 2025 graduation (MDN, 24 June 2025) included the 52nd promotion of officer-engineers, the 10th promotion of the "master complémentaire" and doctoral cohorts. Specialties included mechanical, electrical, chemical, civil engineering and informatique. — [MDN news, 24 June 2025 (search extract)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/actualites/fr/2025/juin/emp24062025fr.php); see also [Radio Algérie, 47th promotion, Aug 2020](https://radioalgerie.dz/news/fr/article/20200816/197832.html)
- The official EMP page (search extract) lists several training offers:
  - a training "ouverte par concours national" to holders of a Master in specified fields, including informatique;
  - a training "sur dossier" for holders of a magistère or an equivalent foreign diploma, including informatique;
  - a "master complémentaire" reserved for career officers who already hold an engineer diploma (informatique is among its filières), described as enabling "le passage du régime classique au régime LMD".
  - The application file for the Master-holder route is a handwritten request, CV, birth-certificate extract, 2 ID photos, copy of the bac, transcripts, copies of the Licence and Master (or Ingéniorat and Master) diplomas, and the Master diploma supplement. — [MDN, EMP page (FR)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_em_anp/sommaire/formation/fr/emp-fr.php); [MDN, EMP page (EN)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_em_anp/sommaire/formation/en/emp-en.php)

**ENPEI "Badji Mokhtar", Rouiba**
- 2026 guide (press summary): a 3-year course with specialization in "Informatique" or "Sciences de l'ingénieur". It requires a scientific bac with strong grades in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, and final admission is "strictement selon le classement par mérite". — [Algerie360, 3 July 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)
- The 21st ENPEI promotion (2022) included the first class of the mathematics and computing ("mathématiques et informatique") track, covering the 2019–2022 cohort. — [L'Expression, 21st ENPEI promotion](https://www.lexpressiondz.com/info-en-continu/anp-sortie-de-la-21eme-promotion-de-l-enpei-rouiba-331720)
- ENPEI graduates earn a DUPCS (diploma of preparatory scientific studies) and can enter the second (engineering) cycle of EMP directly. Most pursue military careers. (UNOFFICIAL/older encyclopedia mirror.) — [fr-academic, Enpei](https://fr-academic.com/dic.nsf/frwiki/583276)
- A Facebook page for ENPEI mentions a minimum weighted average of about 18.5. The snippet was truncated and the year and track are unclear (UNOFFICIAL). — [ENPEI Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/enpeidz/)

**Other branches with adjacent technical tracks (2026 guide, press summary)**
- Naval forces: 5 years (1 fundamental year at Tamentfoust, 3 academic years at Tipaza, 1 sea-application year on the training ships Soummam and El-Mellah or at specialized schools in Jijel and Oran). Specialties include "Télécommunications". — [Algerie360, 3 July 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)
- Air defence of the territory (DAT): Licence in "contrôle aérien et radiotechnique". The article is internally inconsistent on duration (5 years vs "réduit de 5 à 3 ans" after the AMC common year). The École Supérieure de Défense Aérienne du Territoire held a graduation on 14 June 2026. — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/); [MDN news, ESDAT 14 June 2026](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/actualites/fr/2026/juin/esdat14062026fr.php)
- Air forces: officer tracks are Pilote, navigation-aerial operations, aeronautical meteorology, air safety and aviation, over 5 years. No IT track appears in the press summary. — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)
- Gendarmerie Nationale: 5 years (1 at AMC, 3 at the École Supérieure de la Gendarmerie Nationale, Zéralda, for a "Licence en Droit et Sécurité Publique", then 1 at the École de Police Judiciaire, Ysser). No informatique track appears in the post-bac summary. — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)
- Garde Républicaine: 5-year officer training. The guide has separate officer-recruitment pages for the GR, including one for officers. — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/); [MDN GR officer guide](https://www.mdn.dz/site_cgr/sommaire/recrutement/fr/guide_rec_of_fr.php)

**Écoles des Cadets de la Nation (pre-bac pipeline)**
- There are ten ECN lycées (Blida, M'sila, Oran, Tiaret, Béchar, Laghouat, Sétif, Batna, Béjaïa, Tamanrasset). Entry is after the BEM, requiring a BEM average of 12/20 and orientation to "Sciences et Technologie". The 2025 intake required birth in 2010 or later. Both girls and boys are admitted. Selection involves a medical check, a physical test, and written exams in maths, physics-technology and Arabic. — [Algerie360, 13 June 2025](https://www.algerie360.com/ecole-des-cadets-de-la-nation-anp-tout-sur-les-conditions-dadmission-2025-2026/)

**Cybersecurity**
- A presidential decree (JO of 8 June 2024) created an "École nationale supérieure de cybersécurité" (ENSCS) at Sidi Abdellah to train cybersecurity engineers and doctors. Its supervision is reported inconsistently: MESRS only in one source, MESRS and MDN in another. A press item cited about 20 students for the 2024–2025 opening. — [Africa Cybersecurity Mag](https://cybersecuritymag.africa/lalgerie-annonce-la-creation-une-ecole-nationale-superieure-de-cybersecurite/); [Horizons, inauguration of the École Supérieure de Cybersécurité](https://www.horizons.dz/?p=166062)
- The ASSI (Agence de la sécurité des systèmes d'information, attached to the MDN) has run training sessions for state cadres at ENSCS jointly with MESRS. — [APS](https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/actualite-nationale/mpva8cfl-l-assi-organise-la-deuxieme-session-de-formation-au-profit-des-cadres-de-l-etat)

### Inferences
- For a CS profile, the most directly matching military curricula are the AMC informatique Licence/engineer track, the EST Koléa tracks (informatique Licence, Masters in "Systèmes d'Information", "Électronique et Systèmes d'information" and information-systems security, plus electronic-warfare and information/command-systems specializations), and EMP's informatique engineering and postgraduate offers. All three are entered mainly after the bac, except the EMP postgraduate routes.
- The ANP's cyber-related training appears to sit inside EST Koléa (information-systems security, electronic warfare) and EMP rather than in a dedicated military cyber school. ENSCS is a civilian higher-education school with an MDN/ASSI link of unclear scope.

### Gaps
- The official 2026 guide PDF could not be opened directly (mdn.dz returned 503), so the full specialty tables per school were not verified first-hand.
- No 2024–2026 source states EST Koléa's current admission conditions or whether its Licence is still "informatique". The 2019 and 2025 sources differ on cycle length (4 years at EST vs the generic 5 years).
- The identity of the "Central School of Signals and Information Systems" in Echorouk 2026 is unconfirmed.
- Whether Gendarmerie or Garde Républicaine have IT/cyber officer specialties (e.g., for the Gendarmerie's cybercrime units) is not documented in the sources found.
- No source confirmed that EMP's engineering cycle can be entered by a civilian university student in a later year. All sources point to ENPEI as the feeder.

## Q2. Entry conditions for the classic post-bac route (nationality, age, bac stream/grade, medical, marital status, gender, concours)

### Takeaway
For 2026, career-officer candidates needed the 2026 bac with at least 12/20 ("Assez Bien") in Sciences expérimentales, Mathématiques or Technique-Mathématiques, an age of 18–21 on 31 Dec 2026, Algerian nationality, celibacy, and success in a concours with psychotechnical and physical tests. Women are admitted to at least some officer tracks, the Garde Républicaine and the Cadets, and older reporting says EST Koléa is open to both sexes.

### Cited Findings
- The 2026 recruitment is open to holders of the 2026 bac with a minimum average of 12/20 in "Sciences expérimentales, mathématiques, techniques mathématiques (génie mécanique, génie civil, génie électrique, génie des procédés)". — [MDN, Guide 2026 (search extract)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/recrutement/recrutement_fr.php)
- Officier d'active (2026): bac 2026 with at least "Assez Bien" (≥12/20), age 18–21 on 31 December 2026, a competitive exam with psychotechnical and physical tests, and celibacy mandatory for officer categories. — [Algerie360, 3 July 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/); [Echorouk, 15 Aug 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/military-specializations-attract-high-achieving-baccalaureate-graduates)
- Branch thresholds (2026):
  - Air forces: scientific streams with 12/20, weighting Maths, Physics, French, English and PE grades.
  - Gendarmerie: all bac streams, including Lettres and Gestion, with ≥12/20.
  - Garde Républicaine: ≥12/20 and height ≥1.78 m (men) or 1.65 m (women).
  - Military medicine: ≥13/20.
  - Navy, air force, air defence, gendarmerie and GR officer or engineer-officer schools: ≥12/20.
  — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/); [Echorouk](https://www.echoroukonline.com/military-specializations-attract-high-achieving-baccalaureate-graduates)
- EST Koléa (older reports, about 2015): bac ≥12/20 in Sciences expérimentales, Mathématiques or Technique-mathématiques (génie électrique), with a regional-balance criterion. Candidacy is open to both male and female bac holders. — [Ennahar, EST open days (older)](https://www.ennaharonline.com/%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%AA%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%8A-%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7-%D8%A7/); [blog repost, 2015 (UNOFFICIAL)](https://tawdifwa3amaladrar.blogspot.com/2015/07/blog-post_46.html)
- UNOFFICIAL: bac-story lists "original" Algerian nationality, age under 20 on 31 December of the recruitment year, and celibacy throughout training for EST. This is not confirmed by an official source and conflicts with the 2026 range of 18–21. — [bac-story](https://www.bac-story.com/university/speciality/signal-corps)
- Women: allowed into the ANP as officers and NCOs since 1978. — [Carnegie, 2015](https://carnegieendowment.org/fr/posts/2015/11/femmes-dans-le-sanctuaire-des-hommes-le-chemin-vers-legalite-dans-larmee-algerienne)
- Military schools have admitted women for about two decades in specialties including telecommunications and engineering. — [Afrik.com](https://www.afrik.com/algerie-tebboune-nomme-plusieurs-femmes-generales-une-premiere-historique-dans-le-monde-arabe)
- The Cadets schools admit girls and boys. — [Algerie360, 2025](https://www.algerie360.com/ecole-des-cadets-de-la-nation-anp-tout-sur-les-conditions-dadmission-2025-2026/)
- Special forces officer cadets (2025–2026 cycle): bac with ≥12/20, registration on preinscription.mdn.dz. — [Algerie360, 2025](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-ouverture-des-inscriptions-pour-rejoindre-les-forces-speciales-de-lanp-2025/)

### Inferences
- A CS university student who passed the bac more than a year ago, or who is over 21, is effectively shut out of the classic post-bac officer route (age 18–21, and the bac of the current year is required). The "sur titre" route (Q3) is the realistic path.

### Gaps
- The concours written-exam subjects for post-bac officer candidates (beyond "psychotechnic and physical tests") and the detailed medical standards were not found in accessible sources.
- No 2023–2026 official text was found stating, school by school, whether women are admitted to EMP, ENPEI, EST or the AMC informatique track.

## Q3. Routes for students already at university or holders of a Licence / Master / Ingénieur in informatique

### Takeaway
Yes, there are several routes. (1) The MDN runs an annual recruitment of "élèves officiers de carrière sur titre" (university graduates). For 2026, online pre-registration opened on 2 Aug 2026; the age limit is 19 plus the minimum years of study for the diploma (computed at 31 Dec 2026), and candidates must be Algerian, single, pass a concours and be physically and mentally fit. (2) EMP admits Master holders, including in informatique, by national concours and magistère holders on file. These look like postgraduate or doctoral intakes (not verified). (3) The MDN recruits civilian "personnels civils assimilés" (PCA), including Ingénieurs d'État and techniciens supérieurs in informatique, through concours sur titre. No route was found for joining EMP's undergraduate or engineering cycle mid-way from a civilian university.

### Cited Findings
**(1) Officiers de carrière "sur titre"**
- Official 2026 guide text: "OFFICIERS (RECRUTEMENT SUR TITRE) • Avoir la nationalité algérienne; • Etre âgé de 19 ans, majorés du nombre d'années minimum requis pour l'obtention [du diplôme]…" plus "Réussir au concours d'admission; Avoir une bonne condition physique, mentale et psychologique." The reference date is 31 December 2026. — [MDN Guide 2026 PDF (search extract)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/recrutement/images/guide2026_Fr.pdf)
- Algerie360 (2 Aug 2026): the MDN "a officiellement lancé les préinscriptions électroniques pour le recrutement des élèves officiers de carrière sur titre" on Sunday 2 August 2026. Pre-registration is online only, via preinscription.mdn.dz/fs/, and physical dossiers are not accepted at that stage. The age limit is 19 plus the years of study required by the targeted specialty, and celibacy is mandatory. Each specialty has its own criteria, listed in the guide. Covered bodies range from land to naval forces plus the Gendarmerie Nationale and Garde Républicaine. — [Algerie360, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.algerie360.com/mdn-lance-le-recrutement-dofficiers-de-carriere-sur-titre-comment-sinscrire-en-ligne/)
- Echorouk (15 Aug 2026): for university graduates, the age floor is 19, "rising with the number of years needed for the degree", and candidates must be single. Eligible graduates hold degrees from the 2025–2026 academic year. The MDN saw a strong influx of applicants within 14 days, and more than 50 specializations are on offer overall. — [Echorouk, 15 Aug 2026](https://www.echoroukonline.com/military-specializations-attract-high-achieving-baccalaureate-graduates)
- UNOFFICIAL (job-aggregator): university diplomas (Licence, Master, Ingéniorat) are accepted "pour les recrutements directs d'officiers spécialistes dans des domaines comme la médecine, l'informatique, le génie". — [JOBSdz, Oct 2026](https://jobsdz.com/fr/2026/10/nouvelles-offres-demploi-au-ministere-de-la-defense-nationale/)

**(2) EMP postgraduate intakes**
- The EMP page lists a training "ouverte par concours national" to Master holders in given fields (informatique included), a "sur dossier" route for magistère or equivalent foreign-diploma holders (informatique included), and a "master complémentaire" restricted to career officers with an engineer diploma. The Master-holder dossier requires copies of the Licence and Master (or Ingéniorat and Master) diplomas, transcripts and the Master supplement. No date, quota or age limit is given. — [MDN EMP page](https://www.mdn.dz/site_em_anp/sommaire/formation/fr/emp-fr.php)

**(3) Civilian personnel (PCA)**
- In November 2022 the MDN advertised PCA posts for force commands, central directorates and military regions, including Ingénieurs d'État en informatique, techniciens supérieurs and techniciens en informatique, maîtres assistants en informatique, and PEM informatique.
  - Conditions: Algerian nationality; age ≤35 (categories ≤6) or ≤40 (categories ≥7); service-national status in order; a diploma in the specialty; residence in the wilaya of the post; passing the concours.
  - Application uses a form downloaded from mdn.dz (…/recrutement/images/formulaire.pdf), with a deadline 30 days after publication in newspapers. — [L'Express, 10 Nov 2022](https://www.lexpressquotidien.dz/2022/11/10/le-mdn-recrute-des-personnels-civils-assimiles/); [La Patrie News](https://lapatrienews.dz/pour-les-besoins-de-ses-structures-le-mdn-recrute-des-personnels-civils-assimiles/)
- UNOFFICIAL: PCA recruitment is "par voie de concours sur titres ou sur épreuves", with dossier study and interview for "sur titre" posts. Engineer posts include informatique and électronique. — [JOBSdz, Oct 2026](https://jobsdz.com/fr/2026/10/opportunites-demploi-au-ministere-de-la-defense-nationale-algerienne/); [dnalgerie](https://dnalgerie.com/le-mdn-recrute-des-civils-voici-la-procedure-a-suivre/)

**Service national**
- Service national is 12 months (Law 14-06 of 9 Aug 2014: 6 months basic training plus 6 months in units), followed by reserve obligations: disponibilité 5 years, 1st reserve 10 years, 2nd reserve 10 years. — [MDN, réserve page (AR)](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/service/reserve_ar.php); [Wikipedia AR, Service national](https://ar.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9_%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9_(%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1))

### Inferences
- Applying the official formula "19 + minimum years of study" gives these maximum ages at 31 Dec of the recruitment year: Licence (3 years) about 22; Master (3+2) or Ingénieur d'État (5 years) about 24. A doctorate would raise the limit further. This arithmetic is mine, not a published table.
- Echorouk's line that eligible graduates hold "degrees from the 2025–2026 academic year" suggests the sur-titre call targets fresh graduates. Combined with the age formula, a CS student should plan to apply in the summer they graduate (calls open around early August).
- The EMP pairing of "Master holders by national concours" and "magistère holders on file" matches the usual Algerian doctoral-recruitment structure (Doctorat LMD for Master holders, Doctorat en sciences for magistère holders). The EMP route for CS Master holders is probably a doctoral or postgraduate intake rather than entry into the officer-engineer cycle. Whether civilians become officers through it, or remain civilian doctoral students, is unverified.
- The PCA route is a civilian job at the MDN, not an officer commission, and has much looser age limits (35/40), so it suits older CS graduates.

### Gaps
- The official list of specialties for the 2026 sur-titre call (whether "informatique", "réseaux", "télécommunications", "sécurité informatique" or "cybersécurité" appear, and for which branch) could not be read. Algerie360 explicitly did not list them, and mdn.dz was unreachable.
- Training after admission sur titre (school, likely AMC for military formation, its duration, and rank on commissioning) was not found.
- No source documents a "passerelle" or "admission en 2ème cycle" letting a civilian CS undergraduate transfer into EMP, ENPEI or EST mid-course.
- Whether CS graduates doing service national can be assigned to IT roles or converted to career officers was not found.
- Whether women may apply sur titre was not stated in the sources found.

## Q4. Service commitment (engagement) after graduation

### Takeaway
No reliable source found states the mandatory years of service owed after graduating from an ANP school. The statute is Ordonnance 06-02 of 28 Feb 2006 (amended by Ordonnance 21-06 of 30 May 2021), whose relevant articles were not retrievable.

### Cited Findings
- Ordonnance n° 06-02 of 28 February 2006 is the "statut général des personnels militaires". Ordonnance 21-06 of 30 May 2021 amended it: its art. 20 sets age limits and service-duration limits for career military, and it created two new ranks. — [Legal Doctrine](https://legal-doctrine.com/en/edition/le-statut-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ral-des-militaires); [Algérie1](https://algerie1.com/de-la-derogation-dage-dans-lexercice-des-hautes-fonctions-de-la-hierarchie-militair)
- UNOFFICIAL: 2022 pension-related amendments reportedly harmonized the effective-service duration of career NCOs with officers at 25 years, set contract NCOs at 19 years and soldiers at 15 years. This concerns retirement entitlement, not a post-training engagement. — [Khedma News, Feb 2022](https://khedmanews.com/2022/02/%D8%AA%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%B6%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B7%D9%88%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%B5%D9%81%D9%88%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B4-%D8%A7%D9%84.html)
- The 2026 guide press summaries (Algerie360) do not state an engagement duration. — [Algerie360](https://www.algerie360.com/recrutement-anp-2026-le-mdn-publie-le-guide-dacces-aux-ecoles-militaires/)

### Inferences
- Graduates are commissioned as "officiers de carrière" (career officers), so the commitment is effectively a long-term career. Leaving early is likely governed by resignation rules in Ordonnance 06-02 as amended. The exact minimum must be read in the JO text (joradp.dz).

### Gaps
- The minimum service years for officers trained at EMP, AMC or EST, the reimbursement-of-training rules, and resignation conditions were not found.

## Q5. Application process, timeline, portal and documents

### Takeaway

Sub-paths seen include /fs/ for sur titre and /cadets/index_fr.php for cadets. ## Q6. Changes 2020–2026 (new schools, cyber specialties, reforms)

### Takeaway
The main changes found: ENPEI added a mathematics-and-computing track (first graduates 2022); EST Koléa expanded its Masters (information-systems security created 2018, "Systèmes d'Information" Masters graduating through 2025, signal processing added); the 2026 guide formalized the age formula for sur-titre recruitment, online-only pre-registration, and an informatique track at AMC; Ordonnance 21-06 (2021) amended the military statute; and in 2024 the state created ENSCS (cybersecurity), with ASSI/MDN involvement.

### Cited Findings
- ENPEI: the first promotion of the "mathématiques et informatique" track graduated with the 21st promotion (2019–2022 cohort). — [L'Expression](https://www.lexpressiondz.com/info-en-continu/anp-sortie-de-la-21eme-promotion-de-l-enpei-rouiba-331720)
- EST Koléa: an information-systems security Master was created in 2018 (Radio Algérie, 2019). The June 2025 graduation included the 6th "Systèmes d'Information" Master promotion and the 3rd "techniques avancées de traitement du signal" Master promotion. — [Radio Algérie, 2019](https://radioalgerie.dz/news/ar/article/20190722/175241.html); [MDN, 23 June 2025](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/actualites/fr/2025/juin/est23062025fr.php)
- EMP: by 2025, 52 promotions of officer-engineers, 10 promotions of the "master complémentaire", and doctoral cohorts. — [MDN, 24 June 2025](https://www.mdn.dz/site_principal/sommaire/actualites/fr/2025/juin/emp24062025fr.php)
- Ordonnance 21-06 of 30 May 2021 amended the military statute (new ranks; art. 20 on age and service limits). — [Algérie1](https://algerie1.com/de-la-derogation-dage-dans-lexercice-des-hautes-fonctions-de-la-hierarchie-militair)
- ENSCS was created by a decree published in the JO of 8 June 2024 at Sidi Abdellah, to train cybersecurity engineers and doctors. ASSI (attached to the MDN) runs training there. — [Africa Cybersecurity Mag](https://cybersecuritymag.africa/lalgerie-annonce-la-creation-une-ecole-nationale-superieure-de-cybersecurite/); [APS](https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/actualite-nationale/mpva8cfl-l-assi-organise-la-deuxieme-session-de-formation-au-profit-des-cadres-de-l-etat)
- In 2021 the ANP staff hosted the 12th "5+5 Défense" College session on cybersecurity, a seminar rather than a school. — [APS, 2021](https://www.aps.dz/algerie/130715-l-algerie-abrite-le-premier-module-de-haut-niveau-de-la-12e-session-de-formation-du-college-5-5-defense)
- Women's integration is advancing: female fighter and helicopter pilot promotions graduated in 2023 at Tafraoui, and women were promoted to general. — [Afrik.com](https://www.afrik.com/algerie-tebboune-nomme-plusieurs-femmes-generales-une-premiere-historique-dans-le-monde-arabe)

### Inferences
- Cyber skills in the ANP appear to be built mostly through EST Koléa's information-systems and security Masters, EMP informatique, and the MDN-attached ASSI, rather than through a new military "cyber school". For a CS graduate, a sur-titre application in an informatique or telecom specialty, or a PCA engineer post, is the most direct way in.

### Gaps
- No 2020–2026 source found confirms a dedicated ANP "cyberdéfense" officer specialty, or a military cyber school, open to direct recruitment.
- Whether ENSCS admits or reserves places for MDN-sponsored students, and its exact supervising ministries, remains unclear. The primary article returned 503.
